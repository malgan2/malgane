# Syndic de copropriété software and resident apps in Morocco: gap research and adversarial test

Scope: closes the gaps left in `vertical_niches.md` section 6, and tests the earlier claim that syndic SaaS is "under-served". Research date: October 2026. Method: 25 WebSearch calls only. Page fetches and app-store browsing were blocked, so every finding comes from search-result snippets and summaries. Items marked "(search-summary attribution)" were read from a summary, not from the page itself. Vendor and consultancy claims are labelled.

Already known from `vertical_niches.md` (not repeated in detail here):
- Syndic Connect (2020), Thaïs (online payment) and K Syndic exist.
- Law 18-00 was amended (mediation, 8-day notification of AG decisions).
- The syndic profession is unregulated, and unpaid charges are the #1 pain.

---

## Q1. How many copropriétés, residential buildings, syndic firms and volunteer syndics exist (Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Tanger)?

### Takeaway
No official count of copropriétés or syndic firms exists. There is no national registry, and AMCOP has been asking for one since 2022. The best order of magnitude is about **1 million co-owned dwellings**, of which **>40% are in Casablanca** and **half are social housing** (La Vie Éco, undated). That figure is consistent with the RGPH 2024 finding that **24.4% of urban households live in apartments**, up from 17.5% in 2014. No city-level counts for Rabat, Marrakech or Tanger, and no count of syndic firms or volunteer syndics, were found.

### Cited Findings
- **About 1 million co-owned dwellings:** "Il existe un million de logements en copropriété dont plus de 40 % dans la seule ville de Casablanca. La moitié sont des logements sociaux." The publication date was not visible, so the figure may be several years old — [La Vie Éco](https://www.lavieeco.com/?p=17247)
- **About 600,000 households:** Le360's press review of Les Inspirations ÉCO (November 2021) headlines "Copropriété, un enfer pour 600.000 ménages". This is a press estimate, not a census. Unpaid charges are said to weaken "la majorité des copropriétés" — [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/copropriete-un-enfer-pour-600000-menages-250081/); [Consonews](https://consonews.ma/news/immobilier/copropriete-un-enfer-pour-600-000-menages); [Bladi](https://www.bladi.net/coproprietes-maroc-impayes-qui-ruinent-immobilier,109094.html)
- **The two estimates probably differ in unit (dwellings vs households), not in substance** (search-summary interpretation) — [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/copropriete-un-enfer-pour-600000-menages-250081/); [La Vie Éco](https://www.lavieeco.com/?p=17247)
- **RGPH 2024 (HCP census), urban households:**
  - 65.4% live in modern Moroccan houses, **24.4% in apartments** (17.5% in 2014), 2.7% in villas and 2.6% in traditional houses — [Hespress FR](https://fr.hespress.com/401962-rgph-2024-plus-de-60-des-menages-vivent-dans-des-maisons-modernes.html)
  - Another HCP presentation (January 2025) says Moroccan-type housing shelters 73.6%. This conflicts with the 65.4% figure, likely because categories were grouped differently (unverified) — [HCP](https://m.hcp.ma/file/244046)
- **Regional RGPH 2024 data:**
  - Souss-Massa: apartments are 13.1% of urban households; Agadir Ida Outanane prefecture: 22.1% — [HCP Agadir](https://www.hcp.ma/region-agadir/attachment/2801939)
  - Marrakech-Safi: **87.1% of apartments are less than 20 years old** (HCP note, December 2025), so the stock is young and growing — [HCP Marrakech](https://www.hcp.ma/region-marrakech/attachment/2831093)
- **Urban population (RGPH 2024):** 23,110,108, from the sibling notes — [Hespress FR / HCP](https://fr.hespress.com/401944-hcp-la-croissance-demographique-ralentit-a-085-et-le-chomage-atteint-21-en-2024.html)
- **No registry:**
  - The AMCOP president (Dalila Ennaciri) calls for a national copropriété registry and says many buildings are **not even organised as a syndicat** (2022) — [Médias24](https://medias24.com/2022/03/25/un-registre-national-de-la-copropriete-est-necessaire-pour-la-professionnalisation-du-metier-de-syndic-dalila-ennaciri/)
  - Le Matin (2021) also calls for a copropriété reference database — [Le Matin](https://lematin.ma/express/2021/moment-creer-referentiel-co-propris/361209.html)
- **Professional syndics:**
  - "De plus en plus" professional syndics are appearing as residential buildings multiply, but **there is no legal obligation to use one** (2021) — [Le Matin](https://lematin.ma/express/2021/moment-creer-referentiel-co-propris/361209.html)
  - An industry article says most professional syndic firms operate informally, with unqualified and undeclared staff (search-summary attribution) — [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/syndic-de-copropriete-les-professionnels-pointent-du-doigt-les-failles-juridiques)
- **AMCOP** was created in 2021. It brings together professional and volunteer syndics, lawyers, notaries, architects, surveyors and developers. **No member count or count of syndic firms was found** — [FNH interview](https://www.fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/copropriete-interview); [TelQuel 2023](https://telquel.ma/2023/03/10/dalila-ennaciri_1804473)
- **Building size example:** a Marrakech residence of **167 apartments** was in litigation with its syndic (April 2024) — [Médias24](https://medias24.com/2024/04/23/syndic-de-copropriete-des-litiges-judiciaires-rappellent-la-necessaire-organisation-du-metier/)
- **Directory listings of syndic firms** (Telecontact) show small firms. These names suggest syndic firms, not software vendors:
  - Casablanca: K Syndic, Syndic AZ
  - Marrakech: Syndilog, Syndikey
  - Bouznika: GestSyndic
  - Sources: [K Syndic](https://www.telecontact.ma/annonceur/k-syndic/3441855/casablanca.php); [Syndic AZ](https://www.telecontact.ma/annonceur/syndic-az/9022844/casablanca.php); [Syndilog](https://www.telecontact.ma/annonceur/syndilog/3069732/marrakech.php); [Syndikey](https://www.telecontact.ma/annonceur/syndikey/9046907/marrakech.php); [GestSyndic](https://www.telecontact.ma/annonceur/gestsyndic/3396337/bouznika.php)

### Inferences
- **Order-of-magnitude cross-check (assumption-based):**
  - 24.4% × 23.1M urban residents ≈ 5.6M people in apartment households.
  - At about 4 persons per household (an assumption, not sourced here), that is roughly 1.2–1.4M apartment households.
  - This is consistent with the "~1M co-owned dwellings" figure. It excludes multi-storey "maisons modernes" held floor-by-floor, which may also fall under law 18-00 (unverified).
- **Casablanca share:** >40% of 1M gives roughly **400,000+ co-owned units in Casablanca alone**. With half the national stock in social housing, a plausible **mid/high-standing addressable base is about 400–500k units nationally**. This is an inference from the La Vie Éco ratios.
- **Building count:** at roughly 20–60 units per residence (an assumption), 1M units means about **17,000–50,000 copropriétés**. This is a highly uncertain range, given only to frame the market.
- **Market concentration:** the largest digitised syndic firms found each manage about 4,000–10,000 co-owners (see Q2), roughly **0.4–1% of the national stock each**. The syndic market is extremely fragmented.

### Gaps
- No official or press count of copropriétés, professional syndic firms or volunteer-run buildings, nationally or for Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech or Tanger. HCP, the Ministry of Housing, ANCFCC or DGI would be the sources, but none publishes such a count in the search results.
- The date and method behind La Vie Éco's 1 million figure are unknown.
- The share of buildings with a professional syndic vs a volunteer syndic vs none is unknown.

## Q2. Which syndic software and apps exist in Morocco? Pricing, buildings claimed, funding (adversarial competitor hunt)

### Takeaway
The market is **not empty**. There are at least:
- (a) **in-house apps from large syndic firms**: Thaïs/Ténor (70 residences, 10,000 co-owners, online payment) and K Syndic/Kettani (4,000+ co-owners, accounting plus extranet);
- (b) **one small independent local SaaS**: Syndic Connect (Marrakech, 2020, incubator-backed, no funding disclosed);
- (c) **foreign software configurable for Morocco**: Copriciel;
- (d) **developer apps with a syndic module**: Costamar, by Groupe A. Lazrak;
- (e) **custom-development agencies** marketing heavily on "logiciel syndic Casablanca/Rabat" (Kolonell);
- (f) **a few App Store apps of unclear origin**: SyndikB, DIL Group.

**No funded startup, no published MAD subscription price and no player with national scale was found.** Searches found no evidence that "Mon Syndic", "Syndicat.ma", "Wesyndic" or "ImmoSyndic" exist.

### Cited Findings
- **Thaïs** (subsidiary of Ténor Group, a syndic management firm):
  - It launched a mobile app for the **70 residences** it manages and their **10,000 co-owners**.
  - Features: online payment of charges ("une première au Maroc", **vendor claim**), balances, documents and claims tracking. It is on the App Store and Google Play.
  - The launch date was not visible in the snippet.
  - Source: [Infomédiaire](https://www.infomediaire.net/?p=134472)
- **K Syndic** (Kettani Immobilier group):
  - Syndic accounting software plus a resident extranet (accounting, legal and technical information, plus requests).
  - It manages a portfolio of **more than 4,000 co-owners** (self-reported), and presented at ImmoTech 2022.
  - It is a syndic firm with in-house tooling, not a software vendor to third parties.
  - Sources: [H24info](https://h24info.ma/?p=616804); [Telecontact](https://www.telecontact.ma/annonceur/k-syndic/3441855/casablanca.php)
- **Syndic Connect:**
  - Web and mobile app launched in 2020 and developed in Marrakech, for professional and volunteer syndics and syndic councils. Modules include budget management and financial tracking.
  - It is linked to the "Maroc Digital 2025" programme and the EBF incubator in Marrakech. The founder is Yassine Romani (search-summary attribution).
  - The coverage is a **company press release** republished by SNRT News (March 2024) and EcoActu.
  - **No pricing, building count or funding round was found.**
  - An App Store-type listing describes payment-status tracking, claims, broadcast messages and documents for owners; the publisher is unconfirmed.
  - Sources: [SNRT News](https://snrtnews.com/fr/article/syndic-connect-plateforme-de-gestion-de-copropriete-au-maroc-93594); [EcoActu](https://ecoactu.ma/?p=191271); [app listing via mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/syndic-connect/1643767770)
- **Copriciel:** online co-ownership software (administrative, accounting and budget follow-up), **configurable for several countries including Morocco**, with a one-month free trial. No Moroccan client count was found — [Appvizer](https://www.appvizer.fr/construction/asso-proprietaires/copriciel)
- **Costamar** (Groupe A. Lazrak, Martil): a developer-built buyer app with document access, messaging and a **syndic module** (contributions, meeting minutes). It serves one project only — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ci/app/costamar/id6499503514)
- **AMG Building** (Marrakech developer): a construction-progress app for buyers, especially diaspora (MRE) buyers, with milestones, documents and notifications. It has **no syndic functions** — [Challenge](https://www.challenge.ma/suivre-son-chantier-a-marrakech-comme-son-compte-bancaire-la-revolution-amg-building-311147/); [EcoActu](https://ecoactu.ma/amg-building-application-suivi-de-chantier)
- **SyndikB:** a free App Store app with owner financial reports, real-time maintenance tracking, meeting and urgent-issue notifications, repair requests and **owner voting**. The **publisher and country are not stated**, so a Moroccan link is unconfirmed — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ee/app/syndikb/id6474194589)
- **DIL Group Apps:** co-owner financial visibility, **automated reminders for unpaid dues** and document downloads. The market is not identified — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/py/app/dil-group-apps/id6748907472)
- **Kolonell** (a web and software agency; its blog covers Casablanca, Rabat and Tunis):
  - In 2026 it published many SEO articles on "logiciel syndic" for Casablanca and Rabat, selling **custom development**.
  - **Vendor estimates:** custom syndic software costs 180,000–450,000 MAD HT (Casablanca, a 65-residence syndic) or 300,000–700,000 MAD (Rabat), plus 5,000–12,000 MAD/month maintenance.
  - Per-lot SaaS pricing is said to be **2–5 MAD per lot per month**. For example, 4,800 lots at 3 MAD equals 172,800 MAD/yr.
  - "French software adapted" is said to cost about €0.3–0.6 per lot per month, without law 18-00 compliance.
  - All of these are self-described "2026 market estimates", not published price lists.
  - Sources: [Kolonell Casablanca impayés](https://kolonell.com/fr/blog/logiciel-syndic-impayes-charges-copropriete-casablanca-2026); [Kolonell Rabat prix](https://kolonell.com/fr/blog/logiciel-gestion-copropriete-syndic-rabat-prix-mad-2026); [Kolonell appels de fonds](https://kolonell.com/fr/blog/logiciel-syndic-copropriete-appels-fonds-casablanca-2026)
  - Sources disagree on whether Kolonell is Moroccan or Senegal-focused (search-summary attribution).
- **Funding:** a search for syndic or copropriété startup fundraising in Morocco returned **no funding round**. It returned only general commentary on weak Moroccan startup fundraising and the MFounders angel club — [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-financiere-maroc/levees-de-fonds-des-start-up-pourquoi-le-maroc-accuse-t-il-tant-de-retard); [Le Matin](https://lematin.ma/economie/lancement-de-mfounders-nouveau-propulseur-des-startups-marocaines/234710/amp)
- **AMCOP view of digital maturity** (IMMOTECH Proptech Expo, Casablanca, September 2024, where AMCOP was official partner):
  - The copropriété sector **lags in digital transformation and initiatives remain timid**.
  - Online platforms and extranets could centralise information and automate processes.
  - Sources: [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualites-marocaines/amcop-role-technologie-gestion-coproprietes-maroc); [Médias24](https://medias24.com/2024/09/29/comment-la-gestion-des-coproprietes-au-maroc-peut-elle-sadapter-a-linnovation-technologique/)
- An expert argues that a centralised portal showing charges, payment history and works progress would reduce disputes and late payments — [FNH interview](https://www.fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/copropriete-interview)
- **Searches for named products returned nothing:**
  - A combined search for "Mon Syndic", "Syndicat.ma" and "Wesyndic" returned no matching product. Results were unrelated trade unions and directory listings.
  - "ImmoSyndic" was not surfaced by any query.
  - Source: [search results incl. Hespress, Telecontact](https://www.telecontact.ma/annonceur/syndic-az/9022844/casablanca.php)

### Inferences
- **The earlier claim of "only a few small local tools" holds for the independent SaaS layer, but understates in-house digitisation.** The two largest syndic firms found (Thaïs, K Syndic) already give residents apps or extranets, one with online payment. A new SaaS would therefore not win them as customers, and would compete with them for buildings if it bundled services.
- **The real target segment** is the long tail: small professional firms, which are often informal, and volunteer syndics. The only identified independent tool for that segment is Syndic Connect, which shows no evidence of traction or funding.
- **Price expectations** for subscription software are low: 2–5 MAD per lot per month (vendor estimate) against syndic fees of 100–400 DH per lot per month (Q4). **Software would be about 1–3% of the syndic fee**, so revenue may need to come from payment flows or value-added services rather than seats.
- **Kolonell's SEO push** shows that someone sees demand (Casablanca and Rabat syndics buying custom software). It also suggests no packaged local SaaS dominates the query "logiciel syndic Casablanca".
- Absence from search results is **not** proof of absence. Small Play Store apps with no press coverage are likely; app stores could not be browsed directly here.

### Gaps
- No pricing for Syndic Connect, Thaïs or K Syndic, and no building or user counts for Syndic Connect.
- No funding data for any syndic-tech player.
- The existence of Mon Syndic, Syndicat.ma, Wesyndic and ImmoSyndic could not be confirmed or ruled out. Direct Play Store and App Store searches are needed.
- The publisher and country of SyndikB and DIL Group Apps are unknown.
- Whether international players (Matera, Vilogi, etc.) serve Morocco was not tested within the budget.

## Q3. Latest status of the law 18-00 amendment and related texts as of 2026; what they oblige

### Takeaway
Three texts matter:
- **Law 106-12 (2016)** extended the prescription period for charges from 2 to 5 years.
- **Law 30-24**, adopted unanimously by the House of Representatives on 9 July 2024 and reported as in force:
  - mandatory documented **conciliation before court action**;
  - **AG decisions notified within 8 days**, with minutes;
  - co-owners may **convene an AG themselves with 15 days' notice**.
- **Decree 2.23.700** (adopted 21 December 2023) imposes a **standard chart of accounts, budget formats, archive retention and full financial statements before each AG**, reportedly from 1 January 2024.

**No law professionalising syndics** (licensing, professional card, fund guarantees) was found as of October 2026. An October 2025 "barème d'honoraires" decree concerns insolvency syndics, **not** copropriété syndics.

### Cited Findings
- **Law 106-12 (2016)** amended law 18-00 (2002). It extended the limitation period for unpaid syndic charges **from 2 to 5 years**. Moroccan law offers a simplified payment-injunction procedure, though practical difficulties persist — [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/copropriete-reforme-reglementation); [FNH interview](https://www.fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/copropriete-interview)
- **Law 30-24** (amending law 18-00):
  - Adopted **unanimously on 9 July 2024** by the House of Representatives — [ReaConsult](https://reaconsult.ma/tendances-et-conseils-en-immobilier/loi-30-24-copropriete-maroc-reforme-2024-ce-qui-change); a July 2024 press item [Le Desk](https://ledesk.ma/?p=242216) (search-summary attribution)
  - The ReaConsult pages are a **consultancy blog** (2026), not the official text.
  - **Article 13** is amended: a documented conciliation attempt is required before the syndic sues over unpaid charges, unauthorised works, misuse of common parts or rule breaches. Without it, the action is inadmissible. The text reportedly sets no detailed timelines for conciliation — [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/litiges-lies-a-la-copropriete-la-conciliation-devient-obligatoire_XSKXPS7VSVCOFOCQH46GTY6UFU/); [ReaConsult](https://reaconsult.ma/tendances-et-conseils-en-immobilier/conciliation-prealable-obligatoire-copropriete-maroc-loi-30-24)
  - **Notification of AG decisions within 8 days**, with minutes delivered — [ReaConsult](https://reaconsult.ma/tendances-et-conseils-en-immobilier/notification-decisions-assemblee-generale-copropriete-8-jours-loi-30-24); also [Bladi](https://www.bladi.net/maroc-loi-copropriete-devient-plus-simple,109352.html) (from prior notes)
  - **One or more co-owners can convene the AG** without the syndic, with 15 days' notice — [ReaConsult](https://reaconsult.ma/tendances-et-conseils-en-immobilier/convoquer-assemblee-generale-copropriete-sans-syndic-loi-30-24)
  - **In force:** Challenge (relayed by Le360) says the text has entered into force, without an exact date. **Its Bulletin Officiel publication number was not confirmed** — [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/litiges-lies-a-la-copropriete-la-conciliation-devient-obligatoire_XSKXPS7VSVCOFOCQH46GTY6UFU/)
  - Le360 judges the reform "des avancées mais encore des insuffisances" — [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/societe/reforme-de-loi-relative-au-statut-de-la-copropriete-des-avancees-mais-encore-des-insuffisances_VEQOPNBEOZAJ7NL4DV7OAXMY3E/)
- **Decree 2.23.700** (accounting rules for syndicats de copropriétaires):
  - Adopted by the Government Council on **21 December 2023**.
  - It sets a **standard chart of accounts**, a forecast budget, charges and income for the year, budget annexes and a **minimum archive retention period**. Syndics must present **complete financial statements to co-owners before each AG**.
  - It reportedly applies from **1 January 2024** (to be confirmed).
  - Le360 cites the number as "2.23.70", Challenge as "2.23.700".
  - AMCOP warns of procedural-defect risks because no awareness campaign was run.
  - Sources: [Médias24](https://medias24.com/2024/01/14/comptabilite-budget-archives-ce-qui-va-changer-pour-les-syndics-de-copropriete/); [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/societe/gestion-comptable-des-syndics-de-copropriete-ce-qui-changera-en-2024_4HXQDC33FRAKXG2CXANX57VJPE/); [Challenge](https://www.challenge.ma/?p=273791)
  - **Unverified claim:** one 2024 search summary said "the 2023 decree makes digital management tools mandatory for syndics". No other description of the decree mentions this, so it is **likely an over-reading** — [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualites-marocaines/amcop-role-technologie-gestion-coproprietes-maroc); [Médias24](https://medias24.com/2024/09/29/comment-la-gestion-des-coproprietes-au-maroc-peut-elle-sadapter-a-linnovation-technologique/)
- **Professionalisation is still not legislated:**
  - AMCOP calls for a national order and a professional card. Access to the profession is unrestricted, and **there is no control over collected funds** — [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/copropriete-reforme-reglementation); [FNH interview](https://www.fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/copropriete-interview)
  - A May 2025 Casablanca round table debated prerequisites and a "modernised profession", with no adopted reform reported — [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/gestion-de-la-copropriete-au-maroc-les-professionnels-du-syndic-ouvrent-le-debat_R4MJDCYR5FHNTKGUKA7H4BVX2Y/)
  - A 2026 ReaConsult guide lists laws 18-00, 106-12 and 30-24 as the framework and mentions **no licensing reform** — [ReaConsult](https://reaconsult.ma/tendances-et-conseils-en-immobilier/devenir-syndic-copropriete-maroc-formation-guide-2026)
- **Insolvency decree, not copropriété:** Médias24 (1 October 2025) reports a decree in the BO that frames the **appointment of syndics and sets a fee schedule**. This concerns **insolvency proceedings under the Commercial Code** (syndics de faillite), **not copropriété syndics** — [Médias24](https://medias24.com/sujet/syndic/)
- **Conflict on mandatory professional syndics:**
  - ReaConsult's glossary claims that since law 106-12 a professional syndic is "increasingly required" for buildings above 8–10 lots — [ReaConsult](https://reaconsult.ma/glossaire/syndic)
  - Le Matin (2021) says there is **no legal obligation** to use a professional — [Le Matin](https://lematin.ma/express/2021/moment-creer-referentiel-co-propris/361209.html)
  - Treat ReaConsult's claim as unverified practice, not law.
- **Case law, April 2026:** a Nador court order reportedly bars a syndic from cutting elevator or parking access over unpaid charges, with a 200 MAD daily penalty for withholding an elevator card and garage key, based on law 18-00. This is secondhand reporting — [Bladi](https://www.bladi.net/impayes-maroc-justice-interdit-syndics-bloquer-ascenseur-parking,121644.html)

### Inferences
- **The compliance triggers are real but modest.** The obligations favour software:
  - documented conciliation steps (an audit trail of reminders and formal notices);
  - 8-day AG decision notification with minutes;
  - co-owner-initiated AG convocations;
  - standardised accounts and statements before each AG.
- These obligations are not a hard mandate to buy software. **There is no licensing law that would force formalisation**, which was the earlier notes' "professionalisation is coming" thesis. As of October 2026 professionalisation is **still only advocacy**.
- **Recovery is getting harder:** courts are curbing "self-help" sanctions (blocking lifts or parking), and conciliation is now required before suing. This **raises the value of soft collection tools** (transparent statements, automated reminders, easy payment) relative to coercion.

### Gaps
- The official Bulletin Officiel reference and date for law 30-24 and decree 2.23.700, and whether the House of Councillors stage was completed, were not confirmed from primary sources.
- The exact archive-retention period and statement formats in decree 2.23.700 are unknown.
- No draft law on syndic licensing was found. Whether one is in the SGG pipeline is unknown.

## Q4. Payment rails residents use for charges, and the typical unpaid-charges rate

### Takeaway
**No Moroccan unpaid-charges rate exists from any independent source.** The only figure (22%, reducible to 10–12% with software) is a **vendor claim** from Kolonell. Unpaid charges are unanimously described as the #1 problem. Payment-method evidence is thin:
- Thaïs calls its online card payment "a first in Morocco";
- syndics hold bank accounts;
- no syndic-specific integration with CMI, wallets or Cash Plus was found.

General rails are maturing: instant transfers (2023), 16.2M payment accounts (2025), Cash Plus wallet and QR (2026).

### Cited Findings
- **Unpaid charges, Moroccan qualitative evidence:**
  - "De très loin, le non-paiement des cotisations reste le principal problème" (AMCOP president) — [FNH interview](https://www.fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/copropriete-interview)
  - Unpaid charges are the first cause of building degradation (consultancy blog) — [ReaConsult](https://reaconsult.ma/tendances-et-conseils-en-immobilier/charges-impayees-copropriete-maroc-procedure-recouvrement)
  - Unpaid charges affect "la majorité des copropriétés" (2021) — [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/copropriete-un-enfer-pour-600000-menages-250081/); [Bladi](https://www.bladi.net/maroc-gestion-copropriees,88378.html)
- **Unpaid-charges rate, vendor claim only:** software can cut unpaid charges from **22% to 12%**, or to about 10%. Kolonell also says most unpaid charges come from poor information (late calls for funds) and **lack of a simple payment method**, not bad faith — [Kolonell](https://kolonell.com/fr/blog/logiciel-syndic-impayes-charges-copropriete-casablanca-2026). This is unverified and self-serving.
- **French benchmark (not Morocco):** average unpaid rate of about 11%; 215,000 French copropriétés have unpaid charges of at least 20% of their budget — [SeLoger](https://edito.seloger.com/conseils-d-experts/copropriete/assurance-charges-impayees-copropriete-vaut-cout-article-19959.html_3)
- **Recovery path:**
  - Formal notice (mise en demeure), then a payment-injunction application to the court after 15 days without payment — [LesMRE](https://www.lesmre.com/fr/guides/syndic-copropriete-maroc-mre)
  - Indicative cost is **5,000–15,000 MAD and 6–18 months** for a full procedure. The source suggests registering a legal mortgage after 2 unpaid quarters and seeking an injunction after 4 (consultancy estimates) — [ReaConsult](https://reaconsult.ma/tendances-et-conseils-en-immobilier/charges-impayees-copropriete-maroc-procedure-recouvrement)
  - The mise-en-demeure procedure of article 25 is cited as a brake on recovery (AMCOP, 2024) — [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualites-marocaines/amcop-role-technologie-gestion-coproprietes-maroc)
- **Payment methods:**
  - The law lets the syndic **open a bank account**, sign for the syndicat and manage co-owners' money — [FNH interview](https://www.fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/copropriete-interview)
  - Thaïs offers in-app online payment ("first in Morocco", vendor claim) — [Infomédiaire](https://www.infomediaire.net/?p=134472)
  - **No search result showed a syndic accepting Cash Plus, wallets or CMI links** for charges.
- **Typical amounts** (consumer guide, not official):
  - charges of about **400–1,200 DH/month in standing buildings** (Casablanca, 2024);
  - professional syndic fees of **150–400 DH per lot per month**.
  - Source: [LesMRE](https://www.lesmre.com/fr/guides/syndic-copropriete-maroc-mre) (search-summary attribution)
- **Other fee estimates:**
  - 100–300 DH per month per co-owner for basic services — [La Vie Éco](https://www.lavieeco.com/?p=34467)
  - 120–1,200 DH per month depending on standing (a syndic firm's sales manager) — [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/syndic-professionnel-une-activite-en-mal-de-maturite)
  - 8–12% of the annual budget, e.g. 95 lots at a 2.5M DH budget ≈ 220 DH per lot per month (consultancy, 2026) — [ReaConsult](https://reaconsult.ma/tendances-et-conseils-en-immobilier/syndic-professionnel-vs-benevole-copropriete-maroc-choisir) (search-summary attribution)
- **Cash Plus** (2026):
  - Instant transfers to and from Cash Plus accounts launched in H1 2026, plus QR payments at **60,000+ shops** — [Boursenews](https://boursenews.ma/article/marches/Cash-Plus-indicateurs-S1-2026)
  - Its agency "pay with a transaction number" model is already used for subscription billing, e.g. Le Desk subscriptions — [Le Desk](https://ledesk.ma/offres/decouverte/)
  - End 2025: more than 5,200 agencies and 1.8M app users (from sibling notes) — [La Vie Éco](https://www.lavieeco.com/comese/cash-plus-accelere-son-developpement-au-service-de-linclusion-financiere-au-maroc/)
- **General rails** (from sibling notes):
  - Interbank instant transfers went live in **June 2023** — [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/virement-instantane-maroc)
  - 16.3M instant transfers in 2024 — [Hespress FR](https://fr.hespress.com/451874-virement-instantane-lexplosion-des-transactions-en-2024.html)
  - Payment accounts reached **16.2M in 2025** — [Le Brief](https://www.lebrief.ma/le-bilan-de-bank-al-maghrib-pour-2025-100170705/)
- **Diaspora (MRE) owners** complain about the lack of transparency in syndic management. They are a remote-payer segment that needs online statements and payment — [Bladi](https://www.bladi.net/syndics-maroc-mre-crient-scandale,115661.html)

### Inferences
- **The default today is very likely cash collected by a concierge or syndic member, cheques and bank transfers** to the syndicat's account, with manual reconciliation. This is inferred from the absence of any rail-specific offers and Thaïs's "first in Morocco" claim; it was not directly sourced.
- **A practical stack:** a per-unit payment reference across CMI or new-acquirer card links, instant transfer and Cash Plus agency or wallet payment, plus auto-reconciliation. This would be differentiated, and it **directly addresses Kolonell's (vendor) hypothesis that unpaid charges stem from friction**.
- **Remote payers:** MRE owners (diaspora) and holiday-home owners in Martil, Saïdia and Marrakech are a high-value segment for online payment.

### Gaps
- No independent Moroccan unpaid-rate statistic exists, from AMCOP, a bank, HCP or courts.
- There is no survey of how residents actually pay (cash vs cheque vs transfer vs card).
- Whether CMI, a PSP (e.g. Chari, Payzone) or Cash Plus has a "syndic" biller category is unknown.

## Q5. Do property developers, banks or telecoms already offer resident apps that cover this?

### Takeaway
**Partly, and only at the project level.** One developer app with a syndic module was found (Costamar, Groupe A. Lazrak); other developer apps focus on construction tracking (AMG Building). **No Moroccan bank or telecom offer for syndics or resident charge payment was found.** Developer-linked syndic firms (K Syndic/Kettani, Thaïs/Ténor) are the main "incumbent" pattern.

### Cited Findings
- **Costamar** (Groupe A. Lazrak, Martil tourist-residential complex): the buyer app includes a **syndic module** for contributions and meeting minutes — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ci/app/costamar/id6499503514)
- **AMG Building** (Marrakech): a buyer app for construction progress, documents and notifications, aimed at MRE buyers. It has **no syndic module** — [Challenge](https://www.challenge.ma/suivre-son-chantier-a-marrakech-comme-son-compte-bancaire-la-revolution-amg-building-311147/)
- **Developer- or group-linked syndic firms with resident tools:** K Syndic (Kettani Immobilier group) — [H24info](https://h24info.ma/?p=616804); Thaïs (Ténor Group) — [Infomédiaire](https://www.infomediaire.net/?p=134472)
- **Banks:** a targeted search found **no Moroccan bank product dedicated to syndics** (management accounts, charge collection). The only syndic bank offer surfaced was French (Banque des Territoires) — [aides-territoires (France)](https://aides-territoires.beta.gouv.fr/aides/57e6-beneficier-dun-compte-bancaire-pour-syndic-de/)
- **Developer association and DGI:** the DGI built a real-estate price-reference app with the Fédération Nationale des Promoteurs Immobiliers. It is not copropriété management — [FNH](https://www.fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/la-dgi-lance-une-application-dediee-au-referentiel-de-prix-de-l-immobilier)

### Inferences
- **Developers are a channel, not a competitor.** A developer launching a 300-unit project needs a syndic app at handover; Costamar shows the demand. A white-label "resident app at handover" offer to developers (Addoha, Alliances, Prestigia, CGI, regional developers) could seed buildings at scale, but this is untested.
- **Banks:** a bank or PSP partnership for syndicat accounts plus collection is open territory. It also creates a risk that a bank bundles a free portal later.

### Gaps
- No search specifically targeted telecom (Maroc Telecom, Orange, inwi) offers, because of the budget. None surfaced incidentally.
- Major developers (Addoha, Alliances, Prestigia, CGI) were not individually checked for resident apps.
- No check was made of whether any bank's corporate cash-management product is marketed to syndics under another name.

## Q6. Verdict: is the opportunity genuinely underserved, and how big is it?

### Takeaway
**Partly served.** Professionally managed, mid/high-standing buildings in Casablanca and Rabat are being digitised **by the large syndic firms themselves** (Thaïs, K Syndic) and through custom builds (Kolonell's market). The **long tail** of small professional firms, volunteer syndics and the ~half of stock in social housing has **only one visible independent SaaS (Syndic Connect, no traction data, no funding)**, plus foreign software. No funded startup, bank, telecom or developer platform covers it at national scale. Revenue per lot is small, so the market is **real but modest** unless payments are monetised.

### Cited Findings
- **Stock:** about 1M co-owned dwellings, >40% in Casablanca, half social — [La Vie Éco](https://www.lavieeco.com/?p=17247). Apartments are 24.4% of urban households (2024) — [Hespress / HCP](https://fr.hespress.com/401962-rgph-2024-plus-de-60-des-menages-vivent-dans-des-maisons-modernes.html)
- **Largest digitised players found:** Thaïs at 70 residences and 10,000 co-owners — [Infomédiaire](https://www.infomediaire.net/?p=134472); K Syndic at 4,000+ co-owners — [H24info](https://h24info.ma/?p=616804)
- **Software price anchor** (vendor estimate): 2–5 MAD per lot per month — [Kolonell](https://kolonell.com/fr/blog/logiciel-gestion-copropriete-syndic-rabat-prix-mad-2026)
- **Syndic fee anchor:** 150–400 DH per lot per month — [LesMRE](https://www.lesmre.com/fr/guides/syndic-copropriete-maroc-mre); 100–300 DH — [La Vie Éco](https://www.lavieeco.com/?p=34467)
- **Sector digital maturity:** "en retrait", initiatives "timides" (AMCOP, 2024) — [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualites-marocaines/amcop-role-technologie-gestion-coproprietes-maroc)

### Inferences
- **Software TAM** (assumption-based, MAD): 1M lots × 2–5 MAD per month × 12 = **24–60M MAD per year (about USD 2.4–6M)** at 100% penetration. On the ~400–500k mid/high-standing units, that is **about 10–30M MAD per year**. Realistic 5-year capture of 10–20% gives **about 1–6M MAD ARR**, a small business if it sells software only.
- **Payment-flow upside** (assumption-based):
  - If 400k mid/high-standing units pay an average of about 300–500 DH per month in charges (between the 400–1,200 DH standing range and lower mid-market levels), annual flows are **about 1.4–2.4B MAD**.
  - A 0.5–1% blended collection fee or convenience fee would be **about 7–24M MAD per year** at full penetration.
  - Payments therefore at least double the software pool, and payments are where the "unpaid charges" pain is monetised.
- **Service-layer upside:** syndic fees nationally are probably on the order of **1–2B MAD per year** if all mid/high units paid 150–400 DH per month. A tech-enabled syndic (operating buildings rather than selling software), as Thaïs and K Syndic do, captures far more per lot than SaaS. Adversarially, the most successful incumbents found are **service firms with in-house apps, not SaaS vendors**.
- **Underserved, specifically:**
  - a **cheap, Arabic/French, mobile-first tool for volunteer syndics and small firms** with resident payment links, automated reminders documented for the conciliation requirement, 8-day AG notification and decree-2.23.700-compliant statements;
  - a **developer handover channel**.
- **Not underserved:** large professional firms (they build their own) and custom builds for mid-size firms (agencies like Kolonell).
- **Risks:**
  - low willingness to pay (half social housing, informal firms, volunteer syndics);
  - no licensing mandate yet;
  - payment-institution or PSP partnership required for collection;
  - resident trust and adoption depend on the syndic;
  - a large syndic firm or a bank could launch a free portal.

### Gaps
- Actual adoption numbers for Syndic Connect, Thaïs's app or K Syndic's extranet are unknown, as are app-store download counts.
- No survey exists of syndic willingness to pay, and there is no count of volunteer syndics.
- Average charge levels by segment (social vs mid vs high) are unknown, and so are payment-method shares.
- International entrants (e.g. French syndic SaaS) actively selling in Morocco were not checked beyond Copriciel.

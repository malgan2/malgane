# Vertical (industry-specific) SaaS and tech-enabled service opportunities in Morocco

## 1. Traditional retail / hanouts / épiceries: size, tools, gaps

### Takeaway
Proximity grocers ("moul hanout") still handle about 80% of FMCG/proximity trade. Counts range from about 45,000 (narrow, old government figure) to more than 100,000 (2026 trade study), and above 1 million if all small traders are counted. VC-backed players already crowd this vertical: Chari (B2B ordering plus credit book plus a payment-institution licence), WafR (payments/airtime via about 20,000 hanouts), YoLa Fresh (fresh produce B2B) and Inyad (POS). A new pure-SaaS entrant would struggle here unless it rides on an existing distribution network.

### Cited Findings
- Minister of Industry & Commerce Ryad Mezzour (Dec 2024) credited small grocers with about 80% of national proximity commerce. He called each grocer a neighbourhood "small bank" that lends families **840 MAD on average** (the informal "karne" credit) — [Hespress FR](https://fr.hespress.com/399357-399357.html)
- An Aug 2026 Le360 analysis says traditional trade still weighs about 80% of the Moroccan retail market, while organised chains are increasing competition — [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/retail-qui-captera-demain-la-marge-du-commerce-marocain_GVJKWPL2FJCRRFJ5EZPWI5VXGE/)
- A 2026 Ramadan retail study by RED (4,559 grocers surveyed) counts **more than 100,000 neighbourhood grocers**, representing nearly 80% of FMCG sales. It also finds 70% of Ramadan purchase decisions are made in-store — [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/distribution-70-des-decisions-dachat-se-jouent-en-magasin-pendant-ramadan_7KKU3HO54NEWDD4H6QQEWRXNTM/); [Consonews](https://consonews.ma/news/food-boissons/ramadan-70-des-decisions-dachat-se-prennent-directement-en-magasin)
- Conflicting counts:
  - An undated government estimate cited by BNP Paribas gives about **45,000** traditional grocers — [BNP Paribas Trade Solutions](https://www.tradesolutions.bnpparibas.com/fr/importer-exporter/maroc/distribuer-un-produit)
  - An older Yabiladi article (probably pre-2010) gives about 90,000 — [Yabiladi](https://www.yabiladi.com/article-economie-1059.html)
  - LeSiteinfo, citing Hespress, gives 1.3 million "épiceries", which likely counts all small traders — [LeSiteinfo](https://www.lesiteinfo.com/maroc/119127-commerce-moul-lhanout-a-la-merci-des-grandes-surfaces.html)
- La Vie Éco (data as of end-2014):
  - National-plan experts counted **450,000 formal small shops**.
  - A ministry ratio of 27 points of sale per 1,000 inhabitants implies about 918,000 points of sale; a union representative claims more than 2 million.
  - A Casablanca Chamber of Commerce official said **60% have no trade register (RC) and 20% operate without a patente**.
  - [La Vie Éco](https://lavieeco.com/economie/les-epiciers-en-tres-mauvaise-posture-face-a-la-poussee-de-la-grande-distribution)
- HCP (2023) counted about **2.03 million informal production units** (+353,000 vs 2014). Commerce is 47% of them and services 28.3%, and more than half have no fixed professional premises — [North Africa Post](https://northafricapost.com/?p=87417)
- **Chari:**
  - Raised a **$12M Series A (Oct 2025)** co-led by SPE Capital and Orange Ventures, bringing total funding to about $17M.
  - Received a **Payment Institution licence from Bank Al-Maghrib**, the first venture-backed Moroccan company to get one. This enables mobile payments, transfers, supplier settlements and bill payments for merchants.
  - **More than 20,000 shops digitised.** Shopkeepers order FMCG through the app or WhatsApp with 24h delivery.
  - Sources: [AllBusiness.africa](https://allbusiness.africa/insights/chari-morocco-series-a-pi-license); [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/news/feed/chari-raises-12m-for-super-app-development)
  - An earlier bridge round was reported at a $100M valuation — [PYMNTS](https://pymnts.com/?p=1228339)
- **Karny.ma (digital credit book):**
  - Chari acquired Karny.ma in Aug 2021. It was founded by ex-BCG consultants and had **15,000 grocery clients**.
  - Grocers record credit transactions on their phone and **share them with debtors** to collect.
  - Chari said Karny's customer acquisition cost was lower than its own, and used it as a funnel into ordering.
  - Sources: [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualites-marocaines/apres-sa-levee-de-fonds-chari-ma-annonce-l-acquisition-de-l-application-de-carnet-de-credit-karny-ma); [EcoActu](https://ecoactu.ma/chari-ma-karny-ma/)
- Chari opened its first physical B2B e-commerce store in Aïn Sebaâ in March 2023 — [TelQuel](https://telquel.ma/instant-t/2023/03/20/la-start-up-chari-ouvre-son-premier-magasin-de-e-commerce-b2b-a-ain-sebaa_1805348/)
- **WafR** (founded 2021):
  - Raised a **$4M oversubscribed seed in Feb 2026**, co-led by LoftyInc Capital, Attijariwafa Ventures and Al Mada Ventures, with UM6P Ventures and First Circle Capital.
  - Operates through **about 20,000 active hanouts** for airtime, bill payment and digital services. It plans P2P transfers and remittances.
  - Sources: [Daba Finance](https://dabafinance.com/en/news/morocco-fintech-wafr-seed-round-financial-inclusion)
  - Targets 100,000 shops plus micro-insurance and credit scoring (a stated goal, not a result) — [We Are Tech](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/breves/breves-simple/la-fintech-marocaine-wafr-leve-4-millions-pour-etendre-son-reseau)
- **YoLa Fresh** (launched 2023; data as of May 2024):
  - Raised **$7M pre-Series A** led by Al Mada Ventures, with Algebra, E3, Janngo and FMO.
  - Serves **more than 1,000 retailers**, with about $1M monthly GMV and 1,200 t of produce a month.
  - Reports 85% retention and 4 transactions per retailer per week; targeted $40–50M annualised revenue by 2026.
  - Sources: [Techpoint Africa](https://techpoint.africa/2024/05/30/moroccan-yola-fresh-secures-preseriesa/); [Morocco World News](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2024/05/362975/moroccos-agritech-startup-yola-fresh-raises-7-million-to-expand-operations)
- **Inyad** (founded 2018 by Moncef Chlouchi) offers a unified management/POS platform for retail, restaurants and service merchants in the Maghreb/Middle East — [We Are Tech](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/tech-stars/le-marocain-moncef-chlouchi-numerise-la-gestion-des-commerces-de-proximite)

### Inferences
- Chari, WafR, YoLa Fresh and Inyad together already touch tens of thousands of hanouts. The "digitise the hanout" thesis is contested and capital-intensive (logistics, credit, payments licence). A standalone credit-book or POS SaaS would compete with free tools bundled by funded players.
- The credit book (karne) has a built-in viral touchpoint: Karny shared balances with debtors, and Chari found Karny's acquisition cost lower than its own. But it has been absorbed by Chari, which shows the karne is a wedge into fintech rather than a monetisable SaaS by itself.
- 60% of hanouts lack a trade register, and the informal sector is huge (2.03M units). Low willingness to pay for subscriptions is likely, so revenue would need to come from take rates, payments or supplier-funded models.

### Gaps
- No official 2023–2026 HCP or ministry count of hanouts was found. Estimates range from 45k to more than 1M depending on scope.
- POS adoption rate among hanouts, and Inyad's merchant count and pricing, were not found.
- Current status of Karny.ma (still standalone or folded into Chari) is not confirmed after 2021.
- Other B2B ordering apps (e.g., Jumia-like, distributor apps) and their market shares were not researched due to search budget limits.

## 2. Cafés and restaurants: size, tools, gaps

### Takeaway
About 100,000 cafés and restaurants (professional estimate; no official census). The sector was in visible crisis in 2023, with thousands of closures around Casablanca and Rabat. Glovo dominates delivery and integrates with the local POS LaCaisse.ma. Inyad also targets restaurants. Margins are thin, the sector is partially informal and the closure rate is high, so it is an attractive volume vertical with weak economics.

### Cited Findings
- Professionals estimate **about 100,000 cafés and restaurants**. TelQuel stresses there are no official figures and the sector suffers a legal vacuum (May 2024) — [TelQuel](https://telquel.ma/2024/05/30/cafes-et-restaurants-au-maroc-une-crise-difficile-a-mesurer-en-labsence-de-donnees-officielle_1875474)
- 2023 closures and job losses (union figures, not verified by official sources):
  - FNPCRM says **8,964 cafés closed in Casablanca-Settat in 2023** vs 1,870 in 2022, with about 53,800 jobs lost.
  - In Rabat-Salé-Kénitra, 4,769 establishments closed (874 of them before 2023) and 28,614 workers were laid off.
  - Cited causes: CNSS penalties, communal taxes, tax reassessments, raw-material costs and mobile cafés.
  - Sources: [Hespress FR](https://fr.hespress.com/348128-fermetures-de-cafes-en-cascade-et-licenciements-massifs-en-2023.html); [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/societe/fermetures-en-serie-licenciements-a-casablanca-les-cafes-et-les-restaurants-sont-en-crise_HCCWAV4CUZCBPMZUOONWGOAQLM/)
- **Glovo fees (older data):** Glovo had about 1,500 partners and 2,000 couriers in 22 cities (Le360 article, about 2021). It charged an **810 MAD entry fee and 31% commission excl. tax (37.2% incl. tax)** to restaurants. These terms may have changed — [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/dans-les-coulisses-de-la-livraison-des-repas-indiscretions-sur-la-bataille-sans-merci-entre-glovo-et-241945/)
- A 2026 third-party guide says Glovo operates in 38 Moroccan cities with "thousands" of restaurant partners — [Fooderise](https://www.fooderise.com/en/blog/integrer-glovo-restaurant-fonctionnement-commission-2026)
- Glovo has more than 5,500 self-employed couriers (Oct 2024) — [Médias24](https://medias24.com/2024/10/23/sur-le-gril-hamza-naciri-bennani-dg-de-glovo-maroc/)
- **July 2025 Competition Council settlement with Glovo:**
  - Glovo must remove exclusivity clauses from café and restaurant contracts from 25 Sept 2025.
  - It adds more than 35M MAD for couriers.
  - It must publish a guide on how partners are ranked in the app.
  - Source: [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualites-marocaines/glovo-maroc-conseil-de-la-concurrence-accord)
- **Glovo and LaCaisse.ma (Moroccan POS start-up)** partnered in April 2024: Glovo orders flow directly into the LaCaisse.ma POS — [Médias24](https://medias24.com/2024/04/26/glovo-sassocie-a-la-start-up-marocaine-lacaisse-ma/); [Launch Base Africa](https://launchbaseafrica.com/2024/04/27/glovo-and-moroccos-lacaisse-ma-join-forces-for-seamless-point-of-sale-integration/?amp=1)
- Inyad targets restaurants alongside retail and services — [We Are Tech](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/tech-stars/le-marocain-moncef-chlouchi-numerise-la-gestion-des-commerces-de-proximite)

### Inferences
- The end of Glovo exclusivity (Sept 2025) opens room for restaurants to multi-home and for direct-ordering tools (own QR or WhatsApp ordering, own delivery links) to cut a 31%+ commission. That is a concrete value proposition with a customer-facing viral touchpoint: every QR menu or order link exposes the tool to diners.
- The sector's fragility (closures, CNSS and tax pressure) suggests price sensitivity. Products that save money (commission avoidance) or handle compliance (e-invoicing from 2027 for small firms; see section 13) will sell more easily than "nice-to-have" software.

### Gaps
- No official count of restaurants and cafés (HCP has not produced one, per the federations).
- I could not find public pricing for LaCaisse.ma, Inyad or other restaurant POS (Lightspeed, Zelty, etc.) in Morocco, or local QR-menu or reservation tools. The search budget ran out before this was covered.
- Glovo's current Morocco restaurant partner count and commission level were not found.

## 3. Health: doctors, dentists, clinics, labs, physios, pharmacies

### Takeaway
About 18,500 private doctors and about 15,400 private consultation cabinets (Carte sanitaire 2025), plus more than 14,000 pharmacies. Pharmacies are effectively saturated by Sobrus (about 60% share claimed). Doctor software is competitive but cheap (about 200–650 MAD/month), led by DabaDoc for booking and TabibDoc, Cabidoc, WinPlus Med, Iyada and Cabisoft for practice management. The strongest trigger is the CNSS electronic care sheet (feuille de soins électronique, FSE): Kénitra pilot from March 2026, then generalisation. That creates a forced integration moment for every private practice.

### Cited Findings
- **Carte sanitaire 2025:**
  - **34,173 doctors** in total, of which **18,542 are private** (vs 17,213 in 2024) and 15,631 public.
  - 12,303 specialists; 1 doctor per 1,108 inhabitants.
  - Only public dentists are given (458).
  - Source: [Aujourd'hui le Maroc](https://aujourdhui.ma/societe/carte-sanitaire-le-maroc-compte-34-173-medecins-dans-le-public-et-le-prive-en-2025)
- TelQuel (May 2026), citing the Carte sanitaire 2025, counts **15,399 private medical consultation cabinets**. TabibDoc (vendor) claims the Moroccan medical software market grew **more than 35% between 2022 and 2025** — [TelQuel](https://telquel.ma/2026/05/05/digitalisation-des-cabinets-medicaux-un-marche-en-pleine-expansion_1987826). The 15,399 figure came from a search extract; a second search could not re-confirm it.
- **Practice-management software pricing** (indicative, from vendor or vendor-adjacent comparison sites):
  - TabibDoc: a free limited cloud tier (2 users, 500 patients), then **200 MAD/month**.
  - CABIDOC: **200 MAD/month**.
  - WinPlus Med: **399–699 MAD/month**.
  - **DabaDoc Connect Pro: 650 MAD/month**.
  - Another comparison puts TabibDoc at about 300–400 MAD/month.
  - Sources: [Cabdivin](https://cabdivin.com/fr/logiciel-cabinet-medical-maroc); [Zendoc](https://www.zendoc.ma/blog/meilleur-logiciel-gestion-cabinet-medical-maroc)
- TabibDoc Pro covers agenda, patient records, prescriptions, billing, stock, waiting room and online booking. It can run locally/offline and links CNSS and CNOPS insurer data to patient files. TabibDoc also positions itself against Iyada. Sources: [TabibDoc](https://tabibdoc.ma/logiciel-gestion-cabinet-medical-maroc); [TabibDoc vs Iyada](https://tabibdoc.ma/blog/tabibdoc-pro-vs-iyada)
- CABISOFT (NJT Group, Morocco) is another local electronic medical record product — [Brochure](https://demo.njt-group.com/cabisoft/wp-content/uploads/brochure-cabisoft.pdf)
- **DabaDoc reach:**
  - A LesMRE guide (updated Apr 2026) says DabaDoc and Vezeeta are the main active booking platforms, with **more than 5,000 doctors listed** across the two — [LesMRE](https://www.lesmre.com/fr/guides/telemedecine-sante-distance-maroc)
  - An undated interview claims more than 8,000 doctors and health professionals use DabaDoc Pro — [AlloDocteurs](https://www.allodocteurs.fr/sur-dabadoc-on-peut-prendre-rdv-avec-un-medecin-en-30-secondes-41128.html)
  - In 2015 DabaDoc had more than 1,000 doctors — [TelQuel 2015](https://telquel.ma/2015/05/25/dabadoc-service-marocain-sante-en-ligne-sexporte-en-algerie-en-tunisie_1448476)
- **Pharmacies:**
  - Conseil de la concurrence president Ahmed Rahhou (Mar 2026) cited **more than 14,000 pharmacies**, about 1 per 2,600 inhabitants, with the network growing **more than 54% between 2015 and 2024**. He describes a fragile economic model — [Hespress FR](https://fr.hespress.com/466104-466104.html); [AllAfrica](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202603120193.html)
  - An older FNSPM figure was 12,000 — [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/pourquoi-les-pharmaciens-broient-du-noir)
- **Sobrus (pharmacy software):**
  - Claims deployment in **7,000 pharmacies (about 60% of pharmacists)**. This comes from sponsored content — [TelQuel sponsored](https://telquel.ma/sponsors/gitex-e-health-la-fantastique-ascension-de-sobrus_1872385)
  - In 2023: more than 5,000 pharmacies, more than 50% share, 13 African countries, entering France — [TelQuel 2023](https://telquel.ma/instant-t/2023/08/28/healthtech-lediteur-marocain-sobrus-sinstalle-en-france_1827701/)
  - In 2022: 4,000 pharmacists — [Médias24](https://medias24.com/2022/07/06/le-pitch-sobrus-propose-des-solutions-cloud-aux-professionnels-de-sante/)
  - In Feb 2026 Sobrus partnered with **Attijari Payment** to embed e-payment in its pharmacy software — [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/paiement-electronique-et-pharmacie-attijari-payment-et-sobrus-structurent-une-offre-integree_DKKGIQ6UUNAZJO3DAFEP3UNTYU/)
- **AMO / CNSS electronic care sheet (feuille de soins électronique, FSE):**
  - The CNSS initially targeted completion by end-2025. Director General Hassan Boubrik estimated 24–36 months for significant rollout across providers — [FNH](https://www.fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/amo-feuille-soins-cnss)
  - In March 2025 a June launch was announced for consultations and medicines.
  - In Feb 2026 Le Matin reported a **pilot in Kénitra from March 2026, followed by generalisation**. The doctor enters the act or prescription on the CNSS platform and the patient sees it on Ma CNSS. Professionals asked for better preparation and raised questions about integration with the tax system.
  - Sources: [Le Matin](https://lematin.ma/nation/amo-la-feuille-de-soins-electronique-pour-mars-2026/329430/amp); [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/remboursements-tracabilite-fraude-feuille-soins-electronique-maroc-assurance-maladie)

### Inferences
- **Pharmacies:** a dominant incumbent (Sobrus, about 60%, now with Attijari payments), a saturated network (+54% in 10 years) and a fragile model. This is not an attractive greenfield for new practice-management SaaS. Niche add-ons (e.g., AMO claims, inventory financing) might still exist.
- **Doctors/dentists:** many cheap local tools (200–650 MAD/month), and DabaDoc owns the patient-facing booking network (network effects from the patient side). Opportunity lies in **CNSS FSE-native workflows** (claims, AMO reimbursement tracking, e-prescriptions) plus e-invoicing compliance, and in under-served specialties (dentists, physios, labs) where no specific tools surfaced.
- Booking links (DabaDoc) and patient reminders are strong customer-facing touchpoints. But the incumbent marketplace already holds the patient side, so a new entrant should be practice-side first.

### Gaps
- No 2025 counts found for private dentists, physiotherapists, labs or private clinics. The Carte sanitaire 2025 article only covered public dentists.
- Exact current status of the CNSS FSE generalisation after the March 2026 pilot is not confirmed.
- I found no public pricing for Sobrus, DabaDoc's patient-side model or lab/dental-specific software. Vezeeta's Morocco footprint was not detailed.

## 4. Beauty: hair salons, barbers, hammams, spas, aesthetic centres

### Takeaway
There is no census. A practitioner's 2020 estimate is about 30,000 salons, and the sector is largely informal or run by auto-entrepreneurs and feminised. Fresha (global, freemium) already lists Casablanca salons with MAD pricing. I found no evidence of Planity or Booksy in Morocco, and no strong local booking leader surfaced. The beauty vertical has the strongest self-propagating touchpoint (booking links shared on Instagram/WhatsApp), but low willingness to pay and Fresha's free model are headwinds.

### Cited Findings
- A professional estimated **about 30,000 hair salons**, noting salons are not officially counted (2020, Covid-era article) — [H24info](https://h24info.ma/?p=412355)
- The sector is described as popular and feminised, with strong growth in new businesses after 2020, mostly informal or auto-entrepreneur and often seasonal. This comes from a commercial credit site — [Wafir](https://wafir.ma/fr/credit-profession/coiffeur-esthetique)
- **Fresha** has Casablanca landing pages for instant booking, reminders and pre-payment, showing local price ranges (e.g., **balayage 600–1,800 MAD**, keratin up to 1,200 MAD). It claims activity in about 120 countries, including Morocco — [Fresha balayage Casablanca](https://www.fresha.com/lp/it/tt/balayage/ma-casablanca); [Fresha keratin Casablanca](https://www.fresha.com/lp/es/tt/keratina/ma-casablanca)
- **Planity** (France) reported 40,000 partners and presence in France, Germany and Belgium (2024). No Morocco presence was found — [Planity fundraising press release 2024](https://infraviacapital.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/CP-Planity-Levege-de-fonds_2024-1.pdf)

### Inferences
- Beauty has the clearest **built-in virality**: each salon shares its booking link with clients, and clients discover other salons through the marketplace. The French-language Planity playbook (booking + POS + stock) has not been brought to Morocco by Planity itself, which is a possible gap for a Darija/French local product with WhatsApp reminders and deposit collection (no-show protection).
- Risks: Fresha is free for the base tier, and many salons are informal or cash-based. A local product would need WhatsApp-native booking, cash/deposit handling and Arabic/Darija UX to differentiate.

### Gaps
- No official count of salons, barbers, hammams, spas or aesthetic centres.
- I did not find local Moroccan booking start-ups or Booksy data (search budget ran out). The number of Fresha-listed Moroccan salons is unknown.

## 5. Education: private schools, tutoring, language centres, driving schools, crèches

### Takeaway
Private schooling is large and paying: about 1.1M pupils (derived for 2024-25) and about 5,200 private establishments (2019 figure). Preschool is mostly private and now under rapid public expansion. Driving schools face a regulatory overhaul: a new 1,000-question image/video theory exam since March 2024 and a planned stricter licensing regime (deposit 20k to 100k MAD, at least 2 cars). School software is fragmented (OpenEduCat, PraxiSchool, Educap, local custom builds) with no clear Moroccan leader surfaced. Parent apps and student apps are strong B2B2C touchpoints.

### Cited Findings
- **Rentrée 2024-25:** more than 8.112M pupils in total, about 6.976M of them in public schools. That implies **about 1.1M private pupils** (derived, not published) — [H24info](https://h24info.ma/maroc/eleves-etablissements-internats-la-rentree-scolaire-2024-2025-en-chiffres/); [FNH](https://www.fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/maroc-pres-de-8-millions-d-eleves-ont-fait-leur-rentree-scolaire)
- About 1 million private pupils and **5,200 private establishments** (2019 data) — [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/societe/education-voici-ce-que-pese-lenseignement-prive-au-maroc-189555/). An older FNH headline cites "3,168 private establishments counted" (date unknown) — [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/enseignement-prive-3168-etablissements-denombres)
- **Private preschool:**
  - 230,251 pupils in 2021/22, falling to 222,795 in 2022/23 (−3.2%) — [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualites-marocaines/bilan%20de%20l%27%C3%A9ducation%20nationale)
  - 181,639 in 2020/21 vs 212,773 in 2019/20 — [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/laquotidienne/maroc-l-education-nationale-dresse-son-bilan-2020-2021)
  - **Public** preschool units rose from 6,185 to 23,182 between 2018-19 and 2024-25 (INE-CSEFRS/UNICEF) — [Médias24](https://medias24.com/2025/12/28/radiographie-du-prescolaire-marocain-un-progres-rapide-mais-des-defis-persistants-infographies-1603169/)
  - The preschool enrolment rate exceeded 70% in 2025 — [Maroc.ma](https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/le-taux-de-prescolarisation-des-enfants-est-passe-plus-de-70-en-2025)
- **School software:**
  - OpenEduCat markets a Morocco K-12 offer claiming MASSAR (ministry platform) compatibility, MAD billing and a trilingual UI; no price is shown — [OpenEduCat](https://openeducat.org/fr/k12-school-management-software-in-morocco/)
  - PraxiSchool is rated 4.7/5 on 69 Capterra reviews — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.lu/software/33869/praxischool)
  - Educap.io and École Futée are cited as options by a business-plan site — [Modèles de business plan](https://modelesdebusinessplan.com/blogs/infos/comment-gerer-ecole-privee)
- **Language schools:** for a Casablanca school with 1,500 learners, a vendor blog estimates custom software at **120,000–300,000 MAD (excl. tax)** vs specialised SaaS at **2,000–6,000 MAD/month** (2026) — [Kolonell](https://kolonell.com/fr/blog/logiciel-gestion-ecole-langues-casablanca-2026)
- **Driving schools (auto-écoles):**
  - NARSA (the national road-safety agency) launched a study for a sector master plan, citing a growing number of schools and weak training quality. In June 2025 it warned schools against price-cutting ads and urged them to respect reference pricing — [SNRT News](https://snrtnews.com/fr/article/auto-ecoles-la-narsa-rappelle-les-regles-du-jeu-126983)
  - The planned reform would raise the provisional deposit from **20,000 to 100,000 MAD** and require at least 2 training cars per school, pending new texts — [Bladi](https://www.bladi.net/changement-auto-ecoles-maroc,122737.html); [Hespress FR](https://fr.hespress.com/482884-auto-ecoles-vers-une-reforme-de-la-formation-et-des-conditions-dagrement.html)
  - **New theory exam since 25 March 2024:** the question bank grew from 600 to 1,000 questions (950 image-based, 50 video), with an 80% failure rate on day one — [Le Desk](https://ledesk.ma/enclair/nouvel-examen-du-permis-de-conduire-la-narsa-a-la-peine-face-au-fiasco/); [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/societe/permis-de-conduire-un-fort-taux-dechec-dans-certains-centres-dexamen_A3F56R4WWVDMXFVAQKPIOPHJL4/); [TelQuel](https://telquel.ma/instant-t/2024/03/26/la-refonte-de-lexamen-theorique-du-permis-de-conduire-suscite-la-polemique_1863542/)
  - NARSA offered a platform ("Perminou") for candidate training (search extract) — [Infomédiaire](https://www.infomediaire.net/?p=163388)
- **Household education spending:**
  - Spending per enrolled person is 7,500 MAD for the richest households vs 506 MAD for the poorest (HCP, undated) — [Bladi](https://www.bladi.net/tres-chere-rentree-scolaire-marocains,96168.html)
  - Basic school spending is about 2,014 MAD per child (2022, WorldRemit) — [Le Desk](https://ledesk.ma/?p=177742)

### Inferences
- **Private schools** (about 5,000+ establishments, about 1.1M pupils) are a strong B2B2C vertical. The parent app (fees, attendance, grades, messaging) is a daily touchpoint, and fee collection gives payment take-rate potential. The fragmented vendor landscape (open-source, foreign or custom builds) suggests room for a Morocco-native, MASSAR-aware SaaS. Competition intensity is unverified.
- **Driving schools** are a regulatory-triggered niche: the harder image/video exam creates demand for candidate e-learning and mock tests (B2C/B2B2C). The coming licensing reform will consolidate schools and push professionalisation (scheduling, fleet, contracts). NARSA's own Perminou platform is a free substitute for candidate training.
- **Preschool/crèches:** the private share is under pressure from fast public expansion, a weaker growth case.

### Gaps
- No 2023–2026 official count of private schools, auto-écoles, language centres, tutoring centres or crèches was found.
- Market size of tutoring (cours de soutien) was not found.
- No Moroccan school-management vendor with published MAD pricing or client counts was identified (search budget ran out).

## 6. Real estate: agencies, developers, syndics (law 18-00), short-term rentals

### Takeaway
Real-estate agents are unregulated (the licensing law has been pending since 2014) and face heavy informal competition from "samsara" brokers. Syndics (building managers) are also unregulated, while law 18-00 was amended (mediation before court, AG decisions notified within 8 days). Their #1 pain is **collecting co-owner contributions**, and only a few local tools exist (Syndic Connect, Thaïs, K Syndic). Short-term rentals are large in Marrakech (about 9,600 active Airbnb listings). Syndic SaaS looks under-served, with built-in resident network effects.

### Cited Findings
- **Agent immobilier law:**
  - The draft law has been at the government secretariat since 2014; in March 2025 it was described as "soon in the legislative circuit".
  - It provides for a tripartite commission issuing professional cards, mandatory civil liability, a financial guarantee and notary involvement in transactions.
  - Sources: [FNH](https://www.fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/la-loi-encadrant-la-profession-d-agent-immobilier-sort-enfin-des-tiroirs); [Bladi](https://www.bladi.net/maroc-loi-encadrer-agents-immobiliers,113847.html)
  - Professionals denounce informal brokers ("samsara") — [FNH](https://www.fnh.ma/articles/agences-immobilieres-la-profession-unie-contre-l-informel)
  - Agents are associated with anti-money-laundering duties — [Bladi](https://www.bladi.net/maroc-agent-immobilier-blanchiment-argent,96419.html)
- **Copropriété law 18-00:**
  - Parliament adopted a bill amending law 18-00 that imposes conciliation/mediation before going to court and **obliges the syndic to notify general-assembly decisions within 8 days** — [Bladi](https://www.bladi.net/maroc-loi-copropriete-devient-plus-simple,109352.html)
  - One source names this law 30-24, adopted 9 July 2024; this is unconfirmed by others — [Reaconsult](https://reaconsult.ma/en/blog/condominium-law-18-00-morocco)
- **Syndic profession:**
  - Professionals (AMCOP, May 2025) say anyone can create a syndic company: no diploma, no professional card, no solid guarantees — [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/societe/copropriete-ou-en-est-le-maroc_RBUSF4ZLKNAD5GCD7RICLVL5FY/); [FNH interview](https://www.fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/copropriete-interview)
  - **Late payment of contributions is the main obstacle to building management.** No regulation governs the profession and there is no control over collected funds — [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/copropriete-reforme-reglementation); [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/syndic-de-copropriete-les-professionnels-pointent-du-doigt-les-failles-juridiques)
- **Local syndic tools:**
  - **Syndic Connect:** web and mobile app launched in Marrakech in 2020 for professional/volunteer syndics and councils — [SNRT News](https://snrtnews.com/fr/article/syndic-connect-plateforme-de-gestion-de-copropriete-au-maroc-93594)
  - **Thaïs:** mobile app with **online payment of co-ownership charges ("a first in Morocco")**, balances, documents and claims — [Infomédiaire](https://www.infomediaire.net/?p=134472)
  - **K Syndic** (Kettani Immobilier group): syndic accounting software plus a resident extranet — [H24info](https://h24info.ma/?p=616804)
- **Short-term rentals** (Airbtics, Nov 2024–Oct 2025):
  - Marrakech: **9,624 active listings**, 64% occupancy
  - Casablanca: 3,456 listings, 50% occupancy
  - Agadir: 2,464 listings, 53% occupancy
  - Fès: 1,180 listings, 49% occupancy
  - Sources: [Airbtics Marrakech](https://airbtics.com/annual-airbnb-revenue-in-marrakech-morocco-fr); [Airbtics Casablanca](https://airbtics.com/annual-airbnb-revenue-in-casablanca-morocco-fr); [Airbtics Agadir](https://airbtics.com/annual-airbnb-revenue-in-agadir-morocco-fr); [Airbtics Fès](https://airbtics.com/annual-airbnb-revenue-in-fez-morocco-fr)
  - Multiple listings are often managed by the same host — [Aujourd'hui le Maroc](https://aujourdhui.ma/?p=243287)
  - Law 80-14 governs; the tourist tax (taxe de séjour) is charged per person per night; riads and guesthouses need authorisation in Marrakech — [Lodgify](https://www.lodgify.com/blog/fr/loi-airbnb-maroc/)

### Inferences
- **Syndic SaaS is one of the most under-served, high-touchpoint verticals found:**
  - The pain is acute and monetary (unpaid charges).
  - Regulation is tightening (8-day AG notification, mediation) and professionalisation is coming.
  - Few, small local tools exist.
  - Every building onboarded brings dozens of residents into a payment/communication app (B2B2C). Residents who move or sit on other buildings' councils carry the product, a natural network effect.
- **Agencies:** the pending licensing law would create a compliance trigger (professional card, AML) but has stalled since 2014, so timing risk is high.
- **Short-term rentals:** listing concentration (multi-unit hosts) suggests a professional property-manager segment that needs channel/PMS, cleaning and tourist-tax tooling. Global tools (e.g., Lodgify, which publishes Moroccan legal guides) are present.

### Gaps
- No count was found of real-estate agencies, property developers, professional syndic firms or co-owned buildings.
- No pricing was found for Syndic Connect, Thaïs, K Syndic, or real-estate portals (Mubawab, Avito, etc.).
- The exact text and number of the 18-00 amendment and its entry-into-force date are unconfirmed.

## 7. Tourism and hospitality: riads, guesthouses, tour operators, travel agencies

### Takeaway
Record demand: 19.8M arrivals in 2025 (+14%) and 124 bn MAD in receipts, targeting 26M by 2030 (World Cup 2030). New law 80-14 orders (2025) move guesthouses (maisons d'hôtes) to star classification and add mystery-shopper inspections, Airbnb included. This is a quality/compliance trigger. No national count of riads and guesthouses was found. Travel agencies numbered about 1,450 in 2020.

### Cited Findings
- **19.8M tourist arrivals in 2025 (+14% vs 2024)** and receipts of **124 bn MAD (+19%)**, with an official target of **26M by 2030** — [AllAfrica](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202601090010.html); [TelQuel](https://telquel.ma/instant-t/2026/01/06/le-maroc-enregistre-un-record-de-20-millions-de-touristes-en-2025_1968900/)
- By end-May 2025 arrivals were 7.2M (+22% YoY) — [TelQuel](https://telquel.ma/instant-t/2025/06/18/un-record-de-72-millions-de-touristes-au-maroc-a-fin-mai-2025_1938362/)
- **Classification and inspections:**
  - The Ministry of Tourism published **5 orders implementing law 80-14**: maisons d'hôtes, tourist residences and club hotels move from categories to **star classification** — [Maroc.ma](https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/hebergement-touristique-5-arretes-publies-au-bo-pour-amorcer-une-veritable-transformation-qualitative)
  - **Mystery visits** are to become mandatory for all tourist establishments, including Airbnb and informal rentals — [Bladi](https://www.bladi.net/maroc-bientot-visites-mysteres-hotels,115183.html)
- Older Marrakech-only data: about 750 traditional guesthouses, 420 of them classified (pre-80-14, undated) — [Bladi](https://www.bladi.net/maisons-d-hotes-et-riads-hors-la-loi.html)
- The travel-agency federation (FNAVM) represented **1,450 travel agencies** in 2020 — [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualites-marocaines/coronavirus-maroc-la-fnaavm-en-colere-contre-certaines-agences-de-voyages)

### Inferences
- Demand growth plus star reclassification plus inspections push riads and guesthouses toward professional property-management systems (PMS), channel managers, guest communication and tourist-tax reporting. However, global PMS and channel managers already serve this segment, so a local product would need a Morocco-specific edge (tourist-tax automation, police guest registration, Darija/French, MAD payments).
- Guest-facing touchpoints (booking pages, digital check-in, receipts) give moderate virality among guests but limited spread between hosts.

### Gaps
- No national 2024–2026 count of classified riads, guesthouses or tour operators was found.
- No local riad PMS or tour-operator software vendors were identified (search budget ran out). The current travel-agency count is unknown.

## 8. Transport and logistics: car rental, taxis, freight, last-mile

### Takeaway
**Car rental is the standout:** 11,246 agencies, 201,462 vehicles and mostly fewer than 7 cars each, i.e., extremely fragmented. Tourism is booming and a new specification (cahier des charges, April 2024: 500k MAD capital, at least 5 cars, maximum vehicle age) is forcing professionalisation and consolidation. No Morocco-specific rental-management SaaS leader surfaced (unverified). Taxis/ride-hailing are in legal limbo, with taxi-booking app pilots encouraged in 2026. Freight is digitising via the government's online freight manifest (July 2025).

### Cited Findings
- **Car-rental market:**
  - Per the Direction du transport routier: **11,246 car-rental agencies** managing **201,462 vehicles** rented without a driver; the market is very fragmented, with most agencies running **fewer than 7 vehicles** — [Bladi](https://www.bladi.net/000-agences-marche-eclate-fragilites-location-voitures-maroc,118515.html)
  - **1,616 new agencies opened in 2024**; 1,282 in 2025, adding 8,702 vehicles — [Bladi](https://www.bladi.net/000-agences-marche-eclate-fragilites-location-voitures-maroc,118515.html)
  - Professionals bought **more than 52,000 vehicles in 2024 (+13%)**. The FLASCAM (rental-agency federation) leader called 2025 a good year on tourism and domestic demand, and pleads for a new specification — [FNH](https://www.fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/location-voiture-interview-flascam)
- **New specification (cahier des charges):**
  - In force **15 April 2024** (transition to 29 March 2025): **capital of at least 500,000 MAD fully paid**, **at least 5 vehicles per agency**, maximum vehicle age 5 years (combustion), 6 (hybrid) and 7 (electric).
  - FLASCAM says it curbed new agencies, favours large players and that about 1M MAD is needed for viability.
  - Sources: [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/location-de-voitures-au-maroc-entre-essor-touristique-et-defis-reglementaires_OUO2SITHABDMPPXEQ5BNI4AXIQ/); [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/location-de-voitures-les-professionnels-vent-debout-contre-la-nouvelle-reglementation_JRMAYG2NMRGWVBFF2PNFZUZQPU/); [FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/flascam-location-voitures)
  - The Ministry of Transport opened talks with the federation to revise the specification — [Le Matin](https://lematin.ma/nation/location-de-voitures-le-point-sur-les-negociations-du-cahier-des-charges/271108)
- **Consumer side:** average rental is about 654 MAD/day (from 328 MAD/day). Without full protection, agencies block a **10,000–30,000 MAD deposit**. Travellers warn that agency websites' prices are often outdated and recommend requesting quotes by email — [Road Genius](https://roadgenius.com/fr/voitures/location/maroc/); [Routard forum](https://www.routard.com/forums/t/location-de-voiture-a-agadir/7479)
- **Taxis and ride-hailing:**
  - In Jan 2025 the Interior Minister acknowledged ride-hailing apps operate in a legal grey zone and announced a study — [Morocco World News](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2025/01/368006/moroccan-interior-minister-vows-to-regulate-ride-hailing-apps-soon)
  - In Feb 2025 authorities revoked "permis de confiance" (professional driver licences) from app drivers — [Hespress EN](https://en.hespress.com/103329-103329.html)
  - Uber returned in Nov 2025 working only with licensed vehicles — [TelQuel](https://telquel.ma/2025/12/02/seven-years-later-uber-returns-to-morocco-but-taxi-drivers-still-say-no_1962181)
  - In May 2026 the Interior ministry encouraged pilot projects for immediate or advance taxi booking via apps, platforms or dedicated numbers — [Hespress FR](https://fr.hespress.com/473411-taxis-et-vtc-linterieur-promet-une-reforme-encadree.html)
- **Freight:**
  - In July 2025 the Ministry of Transport and PortNet signed a convention for an **online freight manifest**; carriers and drivers will get authorisations remotely, eventually covering all national freight — [Médias24](https://medias24.com/2025/07/17/transport-de-marchandise-le-manifeste-de-fret-desormais-accessible-en-ligne/)
  - The manifest is mandatory under law 16-99, and the ministry is preparing a digital-transformation study — [Hespress FR](https://fr.hespress.com/366514-logismed-la-digitalisation-du-secteur-du-transport-routier-des-marchandises-en-marche.html)
  - Players include Freterium (transport management system; raised from CDG Invest in 2020) — [Financial Afrik](https://www.financialafrik.com/2020/06/03/la-start-up-marocaine-freterium-leve-des-fonds-aupres-de-cdg-invest/) — and Cloudfret (2021, Europe–Africa freight matching) — [We Are Tech](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/solutions/maroc-cloudfret-met-en-relation-expediteurs-et-transporteurs-de-marchandises-grace-a-lia)

### Inferences
- **Car rental shows many signs of a promising vertical-SaaS target:**
  - A large, countable, licensed base (11,246 agencies).
  - High fragmentation (sub-7-car fleets need lightweight tools).
  - Strong demand tailwinds (record tourism, World Cup 2030, 52k vehicles bought in 2024).
  - A regulatory forcing function (cahier des charges compliance, fleet-age tracking).
  - Clear pain points: deposits and fraud, damage disputes, out-of-date prices, quotes by email.
  - Customer-facing touchpoints: online booking links, digital contracts, check-in/out photos and deposit pre-authorisation, which expose the brand to every renter.
  - A possible marketplace/B2B2C layer: inter-agency car sharing and aggregating small fleets for tourists.
- Taxis: regulation is in flux; taxi-booking pilots could open a B2G/B2B niche, but political risk is high.
- Freight: the government's digital manifest creates integration needs for carrier transport-management systems, but funded players exist (Freterium).

### Gaps
- No Moroccan car-rental management software vendors or pricing were identified. I could not verify whether a local leader exists; this is the most important open question before prioritising the vertical.
- No count of taxis or licences, freight carriers or last-mile courier companies was found. Last-mile (COD e-commerce couriers) was not researched due to the search budget.

## 9. Construction/BTP and craftsmen (plumbers, electricians)

### Takeaway
Activity here is marketplace-led (Saweblia, Bricall, Bricool) rather than SaaS. Those players are small (200–400 artisans) and pivoting to B2B maintenance contracts. No quoting or invoicing SaaS for craftsmen surfaced. The sector is highly informal, with low SaaS readiness.

### Cited Findings
- **Saweblia** (Casablanca) connects clients with electricians, plumbers and other trades. It runs on the web or WhatsApp with no app, sends quotes within 20 minutes to 48 hours, and has **more than 200 artisans** with under 2h average arrival. It is pivoting to B2B maintenance contracts. Founding year is reported as 2015 or 2019 depending on the source — [We Are Tech](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/solutions/maroc-saweblia-numerise-le-secteur-des-travaux-a-domicile); [H24info](https://h24info.ma/?p=752561)
- **Bricall** (Digiserv) is reported to cover 14 trades in 9 cities with more than 400 artisans (Moroccan press; exact page not opened) — [Consonews](https://consonews.ma/?p=35844)
- **Bricool.ma** launched in 2018 as an artisan–client marketplace with mutual ratings — [Le Matin](https://lematin.ma/express/2018/lancement-plateforme-bricool/299645.html)
- **Artisway**, a government app launched June 2026 to promote Moroccan handicraft (craft shops, not trades) — [TelQuel](https://telquel.ma/instant-t/2026/06/19/une-nouvelle-application-mobile-pour-renforcer-la-promotion-de-lartisanat_1995055/); [Ministry call for expressions of interest](https://mda.gov.ma/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/AMI_ARTISWAY.pdf)

### Inferences
- Several marketplaces since 2016–2018 have stayed small, which suggests weak unit economics in B2C home services. Saweblia's pivot to B2B maintenance hints that demand from businesses and syndics (building maintenance) may be stronger, a possible adjacency to syndic SaaS.
- A craftsman quoting/invoicing tool (WhatsApp-native) has a customer-facing touchpoint (every quote or invoice is seen by a client). But informality and the e-invoicing date for small firms (2027) limit near-term pull.

### Gaps
- No counts of construction SMEs or craftsmen were found, and no BTP SaaS (estimating, site management) vendors were identified. Search budget was exhausted before this was covered.

Gyms are a small, concentrated vertical (more than 545 clubs listed; City Club about 100 sites; Smart Fit entering), where chains build their own tools. Weddings are a big-ticket, fragmented and review-driven market (an estimated **30–35 bn MAD/year**) with no consolidated data or obvious software leader. That suggests a marketplace/booking and vendor-CRM opportunity with strong social virality.

- **Gyms:**

They are discovered via Instagram/WhatsApp and reviews, which fits a booking-plus-deposit-plus-contract tool with public vendor pages (virality through guests and families). The market lacks structured data, which is both an opportunity and a sign of informality.

### Gaps
- No counts were found of caterers, neggafas, event halls or photographers. No Moroccan wedding marketplace or vendor-software players were identified.
- No gym-management SaaS players or prices for Morocco were found.

### Takeaway
65,315 active cooperatives (end-2025) with about 789,000 members, but only 333 are "large". Total turnover is only about 14 bn MAD, so the average cooperative is tiny. This is a B2G/donor-funded (ODCo, ministry, international cooperation) opportunity more than a self-serve SaaS market.

- At the closing of the International Year of Cooperatives 2025 (with ODCo, the cooperative development office):
  - **65,315 active cooperatives**, about **789,000 members** (more than 272,000 women, 18,000 youth).
  - **333 large cooperatives**, about **14 bn MAD turnover** and about 120,000 direct jobs.
  - The social & solidarity economy created 24,558 jobs in 2025.
  - Sources: [Maroc.ma](https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/le-secteur-de-leconomie-sociale-et-solidaire-contribue-la-creation-de-plus-de-24500-emplois-en-2025); [Hespress EN](https://en.hespress.com/130581-minister-moroccos-social-solidarity-economy-sector-helps-create-over-24500-jobs-in-2025)
- The social-economy strategy targets 50,000 jobs a year and 8% of GDP by 2035 — [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/essor-de-leconomie-sociale-vers-50000-emplois-annuels-et-8-du-pib-dici-2035_VJFR7WAWPBEHVB5TDQTW3UADP4/)
- ODCo has programmes to help youth cooperatives develop — [Hespress FR](https://fr.hespress.com/480530-comment-lodco-veut-donner-aux-cooperatives-de-jeunes-les-moyens-de-se-developper.html)
- YoLa Fresh (see section 1) connects smallholder farmers to traditional retailers — [Techpoint Africa](https://techpoint.africa/2024/05/30/moroccan-yola-fresh-secures-preseriesa/)

### Inferences
- Average turnover is about 214,000 MAD per cooperative (14 bn / 65,315; my calculation, not published). Most cooperatives cannot pay meaningful SaaS fees. Viable models are government/donor-funded tools (governance, bookkeeping, traceability for argan and other labels) or marketplace/export channels that take a commission.

### Gaps
- No breakdown of cooperatives by sector (argan, dairy, etc.) or their digital tooling was found. Agritech SaaS players beyond YoLa Fresh were not researched.

## 12. Professional services: lawyers, notaries, accountants, architects

### Takeaway
These are small, regulated professions: about 15,000 lawyers (2019), 400–800 chartered accountants, and notaries in the low thousands at most (440 in 2003). Lawyer professional reform (draft law 66.23) is contested. The bigger opportunity is to treat accountants as a **distribution channel** for SME e-invoicing and bookkeeping SaaS (only 2% of companies have certified accounts), rather than to sell practice software to the professions themselves.

### Cited Findings
- **Lawyers:** about **15,000 lawyers** and 4,575 trainee lawyers in 2019 — [Médias24](https://www.medias24.com/barreaux-4-575-futurs-avocats-la-profession-s-inquiete-3679.html)
  - The bar association (ABAM) grouped 17 bars in 2024; Casablanca, the largest, is outside it — [Hespress FR](https://fr.hespress.com/392966-392966.html)
  - Lawyers protested draft law 66.23 reforming the profession — [Hespress FR](https://fr.hespress.com/458747-organisation-de-la-profession-davocat-ce-que-prevoit-le-projet-de-loi-66-23.html)
- **Notaries:** 440 in 2003, plus 1,500 clerks/trainees (very old data) — [Le notariat marocain (PDF)](https://www.ramajudicial.pr/Documentos/EvaluacionFuncionNotarial/Maroc-LE-NOTARIAT-MAROCAIN.pdf)
- **Chartered accountants (Ordre des experts-comptables):**
  - **400 registered members** across 22 cities as of Aug 2026, per a private directory — [LesMRE](https://www.lesmre.com/fr/guides/expert-comptable-maroc-verifier-choisir-depuis-etranger)
  - Contradicted by "more than 800" (undated) — [EcoActu](https://ecoactu.ma/?p=80411)
  - The profession's head is quoted (Apr 2026) as saying only **2% of companies have certified accounts** — [Médias24](https://medias24.com/2026/04/12/faycal-mekouar-2-des-entreprises-seulement-ont-des-comptes-certifies-est-ce-normal-1658424/)

### Inferences
- These professions are too small for a large self-serve vertical SaaS. Accountants and "comptables agréés" (approved bookkeepers, count unknown) are the gatekeepers of SME bookkeeping and will be central to the 2026–2027 e-invoicing rollout, which makes them high-leverage resellers or partners for SME SaaS.

### Gaps
- No current counts were found for lawyers (2024–2026), notaries, architects or comptables agréés. No legal or notarial practice software players or pricing were found.

## 13. Cross-cutting: regulatory triggers, network effects, most underserved and promising verticals

### Takeaway
On the evidence gathered, the most promising under-served verticals are:
1. **Syndics de copropriété** — acute payment-collection pain, no profession regulation, law 18-00 tightening, few small local tools, strong resident network effects.
2. **Car-rental agencies** — 11,246 fragmented agencies, a compliance-forcing specification, a tourism boom, customer-facing contracts and booking links; the competitive landscape is still unverified.
3. **Private schools and driving schools** — paying B2B2C base, regulatory change in driving schools.
4. **Beauty and wedding vendors** — the highest virality, but the most informal.

Health (doctors) has a clear trigger (CNSS electronic care sheet) but is already served by cheap local tools. Pharmacies are effectively locked up by Sobrus, and the hanout space is crowded by well-funded fintech/B2B players. **E-invoicing (small firms from 1 Jan 2027, pending decree)** is a universal forcing function that any vertical SaaS can bundle.

### Cited Findings
- **E-invoicing calendar:**

The model uses DGI clearance and the UBL format — [Upsilon Consulting](https://upsilon-consulting.com/facturation-electronique-maroc-2026/); [Edicom](https://edicomgroup.com/fr/blog/maroc-obligation-facturation-electronique)

- The verticals with the **strongest self-propagation** are syndics (residents), beauty (client booking links), restaurants (QR menus/order links), schools (parents), car rental (renters' booking and contract links) and wedding vendors (guests and reviews).

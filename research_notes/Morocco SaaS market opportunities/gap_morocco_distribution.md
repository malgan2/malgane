# Gap research: how Moroccan B2B software startups acquired customers, and Morocco-specific distribution channels

*Method note: WebFetch is blocked in this environment, so every finding below comes from search-engine result summaries, not full-page reads. I used the full budget of 25 searches. Where a summary did not say which of several listed pages held a fact, I cite the most likely page or pages and flag it. Company figures are self-reported unless stated otherwise. This file adds to `distribution_and_viral_growth.md` and does not repeat what is there: YouCan's affiliate programme and events, Chari and Orange, the Mowakaba claims from consultancies, the Commerce 2030 deal with Woliz, and the CMI/interchange context. ## 1. How did Sobrus, DabaDoc, Inyad, Chari, YouCan, Sendit, LaCaisse and Facturis acquire their first 1,000 customers (channels, CAC, sales team size)?

### Takeaway
No founder interview I found gives a CAC, a channel mix or a sales headcount. The qualitative record is consistent: Moroccan B2B pioneers won their first customers through **slow, in-person, founder-led field selling**, not self-serve or paid digital. Chari visited shops door to door (about 50,000 stores claimed). DabaDoc pitched doctors one by one for about 18 months before charging. LaCaisse.ma took about 5 years and about 15 staff to reach about 1,000 POS clients. Reaching 1,000 paying customers typically took **2–5 years**. YouCan is the exception: it passed 1,000 merchants in its first year through the e-commerce/COD community.

### Cited Findings
**Chari (hanout B2B ordering → fintech)**
- Chari's team "sillonné les rues", visiting shop after shop to win the trust of about **50,000 merchants**. The headline calls Ismael Belkhayat "l'entrepreneur marocain qui a visité 50 000 magasins" (May 2024). *Flag: the indexed page title reads "Pas de résultat", so the article may no longer be online; the detail comes from the search summary.* — [Managers.tn](https://managers.tn/2024/05/20/rencontrez-ismael-belkhayat-lentrepreneur-marocain-qui-a-visite-50-000-magasins)
- In a podcast, the founder framed the mission as making life easier for **45,000 Moroccan épiciers**, who struggle to manage restocking while serving customers. — [Histoires d'entreprises podcast #64](https://podcast.ausha.co/histoires-d-entreprises/64-ismael-belkhayat-co-fondateur-et-ceo-de-chari)
- The wedge was operational, not software: **free delivery in under 24 hours** to épiceries ordering through the app, more than **5,000 monthly active épiceries**, and seven new warehouses opened at once (including Marrakech, Agadir and Meknès). The article's date was not shown; it is probably 2021–2022. — [Finances News](https://fnh.ma/article/actualites-marocaines/e-commerce-chari-annonce-l-extension-de-son-activite-a-l-ensemble-du-territoire-marocain)

**DabaDoc (doctor booking; Orange/AXA-owned since 2021)**
- It launched to doctors in **October 2013** and to the public in January 2014. — [TelQuel 2014](https://telquel.ma/2014/02/11/dabadoc-lapplication-de-prise-de-rendez-vous-medical-en-ligne_11040); [TelQuel 2015](https://telquel.ma/2015/05/25/dabadoc-service-marocain-sante-en-ligne-sexporte-en-algerie-en-tunisie_1448476)

- Traction: **more than 1,000 registered doctors** (TelQuel) and **more than 2,000 doctors across Morocco, Algeria and Tunisia by May 2015** (Wamda). — [TelQuel 2015](https://telquel.ma/2015/05/25/dabadoc-service-marocain-sante-en-ligne-sexporte-en-algerie-en-tunisie_1448476); [Wamda 2015](https://www.wamda.com/en/2015/05/dabadoc-expands-tunisia-and-algeria)

That is a 50% discount for annual prepayment (2015 data; the summary did not say which of the two pages held the figures). — [TelQuel 2015](https://telquel.ma/2015/05/25/dabadoc-service-marocain-sante-en-ligne-sexporte-en-algerie-en-tunisie_1448476); [Wamda 2015](https://www.wamda.com/en/2015/05/dabadoc-expands-tunisia-and-algeria)

**YouCan (e-commerce/COD platform)**
- Founded in 2020 and led by Mohammed El Ghaissani, it passed **1,000 affiliated merchants and retailers** in its first year and planned MENA expansion. **Sendit, Inyad, Facturis**
- **Sendit** distributes through a **free Shopify app** that creates shipments from orders and prints labels; Sendit bills its fees separately. No founder, funding or seller-count data was found. — [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/sendit)
- **Inyad:** no 2025–26 user counts, funding or acquisition data found. Its visible channel is French SEO content on the e-invoicing calendar. — [Inyad blog](https://inyad.com/blog/calendrier-facture-electronique-maroc-2026-2028)
- **Facturis:** no acquisition data found (search budget).

**Related: who holds the "50,000 shops by 2030" government deal**
- In September 2025, at the announcement of the 2026 Assises du Commerce, the Ministry of Industry and Trade signed two agreements, including one with **z.systems to equip up to 50,000 small shops with digital solutions by 2030**. This resolves an open gap in the earlier notes; Woliz holds the other known Commerce 2030 deal (20,000 shops). — [TelQuel](https://telquel.ma/instant-t/2025/09/30/mezzour-annonce-la-tenue-des-assises-du-commerce-en-2026_1953035/); [Médias24](https://medias24.com/2025/09/30/face-a-lurgence-de-la-modernisation-le-maroc-mise-sur-des-assises-du-commerce/)

**Funding context (conflict)**
- Partech Africa: Moroccan startups raised **$82M in 2024, −11% vs 2023**, while deal count rose sharply. — [Hespress FR](https://fr.hespress.com/408666-408666.html). This conflicts with the earlier note's figure of about $95M across 40 deals in 2024, from a blog roundup ([mean.ceo](https://blog.mean.ceo/startups-morocco-news-june-2026/)).

### Inferences
- In every documented case, getting to 1,000 customers was **labour-intensive and slow**: DabaDoc took about 18 months free before charging, and LaCaisse took about 5 years to reach about 1,000 clients with about 15 staff. Plan for a field or inside-sales layer and multi-year timelines. Do not assume viral or PLG adoption.
- The fastest growers attached to **an existing flow**: Chari to restocking with free delivery, YouCan to the COD e-commerce community, LaCaisse to Glovo orders, Sendit to Shopify stores. Chari, YouCan and Sendit all monetise through transactions or GMV rather than software seats.
- DabaDoc's 2015 pricing (225 vs 450 MAD/month) suggests **steep annual-prepay discounts** have long been used to reduce churn and collection friction in Morocco.

### Gaps
- No CAC, sales headcount or channel-mix figures for any of the eight companies. Founders' podcasts (Chari on Ausha) may contain them but could not be fetched.
- No data on whether Sobrus used pharma wholesalers or pharmacist bodies (Ordre / syndicats) as channels.
- No data on Sendit's founders, funding or seller base; no Inyad or Facturis user counts for 2025–26.
- Chari's field sales headcount and current agent model are unconfirmed.

## 2. Maroc PME and government programmes (Mowakaba, Istitmar, Tatwir, "digital" vouchers): official 2025–2026 terms, and can SaaS vendors be listed providers?

### Takeaway
I could **not find any official Mowakaba text, provider list or "digital voucher"** in 2025–2026 coverage. The only official subsidy framework found is the **June 2025 TPME support decree (no. 2.25.342)**. It offers **up to 30% of project cost** (caps of 500k MAD for TPE and 2M MAD for PME, minimum 10% own funds) for investment, modernisation *and digital transformation*. That is far from the **80–90%** claimed by integrators in the earlier notes, which should now be treated as unverified and possibly outdated. Nothing indicates a provider-listing process that SaaS vendors could join. Digital Morocco 2030 helps the **vendor** side (PME Tech label, innovation grants), and the ADD runs awareness and diagnostic networks (DigiTPME, Moukawala Raqmiya), not purchase vouchers.

### Cited Findings
**TPME direct-subsidy decree (Investment Charter, 2025)**
- Decree **2.25.342 of 12 June 2025** created direct subsidies for private TPME covering **investments, modernisation and digital transformation**. It was activated after four Head-of-Government decisions were published in the Bulletin Officiel; these set eligible activities by region, priority activities and required documents. *Attribution among these three pages is approximate.* — [Hespress FR](https://fr.hespress.com/449569-449569.html); [Le Matin](https://lematin.ma/economie/subventions-aux-tpme-ce-que-vous-pouvez-obtenir-comment-en-beneficier/272979/amp); [Finances News](https://www.fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/soutien-entreprises-tpme)
- The public subsidy is **up to 30% of total project cost**. One source mentions up to 40% for projects carried out as a group (the summary is ambiguous). Caps are **500,000 MAD (TPE)** and **2M MAD (PME)**; firms must self-fund at least **10%**. Extra premiums apply for stable job creation, investment outside traditional zones and primary activities (agriculture). — same three sources as above.
- No 2026 update to these conditions and no digital-specific rate were found.

**Mowakaba**
- The only 2025–26 mention retrieved is a **vendor blog (Inyad)**. It says MOWAKABA, "porté par le Ministère de l'Industrie et du Commerce", subsidises SME digitalisation including tax-compliance (e-invoicing) projects, and that "a significant share" of equipment cost may be covered, case by case. — [Inyad blog](https://inyad.com/blog/choisir-logiciel-facturation-conforme-dgi)
- A targeted search for Mowakaba beneficiaries or results for 2024–2025 returned **nothing official**. — [search result set incl. FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-entreprises/digitalisation-des-pme-un-bilan-encore-loin-du-compte)
- **"Tatwir" and "Istitmar" were not confirmed** as current digitalisation programmes in any 2025–26 result. — [search result set incl. maroc.ma](https://www.maroc.ma/fr/actualites/lancement-du-reseau-digitpme-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-digitale-des-tpme)

**Digital Morocco 2030 / ADD instruments**
- The strategy was launched on **25 September 2024**, targeting **240,000 jobs and 100B MAD**. — [TelQuel](https://telquel.ma/instant-t/2024/09/25/digital-morocco-2030-ce-que-promet-ghita-mezzour-pour-leconomie-numerique-et-la-digitalisation-des-services-publics_1895253/); [Hespress FR](https://fr.hespress.com/387500-387500.html)
- Its "Digital Enterprise" component creates a **PME Tech label** giving innovative tech firms **national preference in public procurement**, plus **grants for Moroccan tech companies to build innovative solutions** (for example, an SME's AI solution). This is a supply-side benefit for a SaaS vendor, not a demand subsidy for its customers. A separate programme to support TPME digital transition was announced. — [Hespress FR (Mezzour)](https://fr.hespress.com/387753-digital-morocco-2030-mezzour-confirme-la-place-des-tpme-et-pme-avec-un-label-tech-et-des-subventions-innovantes.html)
- **DigiTPME network** launched in Marrakech (April 2026) under Maroc Digital 2030 to digitise TPME; TPME are more than 95% of the economic fabric. — [maroc.ma (MAP)](https://www.maroc.ma/fr/actualites/lancement-du-reseau-digitpme-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-digitale-des-tpme)
- The ADD's 2024 plan has two flagship TPME programmes, **DigiTPME** and **Moukawala Raqmiya**. The latter is an online **self-diagnostic of digital maturity**, not a subsidy. — [EcoActu](https://ecoactu.ma/moukawala-raqmiya-gitex-africa-morocco/)
- An Officium/ONPM study found **30% of TPME active in digital projects and 30% doing nothing** (date unclear; the summary did not say which of these two pages held it). — [Finances News](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-entreprises/transformation-digitale-les-pme-ont-du-mal); [La Vie Éco](https://www.lavieeco.com/tech/transformation-digitale-levier-pour-les-tpme/)
- The World Bank committed **$250M** to Morocco's digital programme (headline only). — [Hespress FR](https://fr.hespress.com/451343-banque-mondiale-250-m-pour-propulser-le-chantier-digital-du-maroc.html)

**Charte TPE (December 2025)**
- Signed **4 December 2025** by the Ministry of Economy and Finance, Bank Al-Maghrib, Tamwilcom, **Maroc PME**, GPBM (the banks' association), FNAM and CGEM. Commitments: a **national TPE credit score from 2026** built into banks' rating systems, and coordinated training, awareness and technical support. A **Comité TPE** under BAM supervision publishes an annual report. BAM has offered a preferential **TPE refinancing line since March 2025**. — [maroc.ma](https://www.maroc.ma/fr/actualites/rabat-signature-de-la-charte-relative-au-financement-et-laccompagnement-des-tpe); [Finances News](https://www.fnh.ma/article/actualite-financiere-maroc/charte-tpe-signature-rabat); [H24info](https://h24info.ma/economie/charte-des-tpe-financement-et-accompagnement/)

**Foreign-currency rules (relevant to foreign SaaS competitors and to Moroccan vendors' own tooling)**
- Moroccan entities without an FX account may pay **imported services by international card up to 200,000 MAD per calendar year per beneficiary**, linked to tax paid in the last closed year (IGOC 2026). **JEI-labelled** innovative startups may pay up to **2M MAD/year**, through the Startup Hub portal (up from 500k MAD in 2019 sources). — [Office des Changes FAQ](https://www.oc.gov.ma/fr/faq/les-entreprises-marocaines-peuvent-elles-regler-par-carte-de-paiement-internationale-leurs); [Finances News](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-entreprises/paiement-en-devises-l-office-des-changes-desserre-l-etau-sur-les-start-up)

### Inferences
- The earlier recommendation, to get onto the Mowakaba list so integrators can sell packs at 10–20% of list price, **is not supported by any official source found**. The verifiable subsidy (30%, project-based, capex-oriented, with documentation burden) suits larger integrated projects such as ERP plus hardware, not 100–300 MAD/month SaaS.
- The **Charte TPE credit score** (from 2026) is a possible hook: software that produces clean, bank-readable financial records could become a pathway to TPE credit. That gives banks a reason to push it, but it also explains why banks build their own tools (see Q3).
- The PME Tech label and innovation grants are useful **for the vendor** (public-procurement preference, R&D money), not as a customer-acquisition channel.
- The FX card limit (200k MAD/year) does not block most SMEs from buying foreign SaaS (Zoho, Odoo Online, Shopify), but it adds friction. That favours local MAD-billed tools somewhat.

### Gaps
- Official Maroc PME Mowakaba terms (rate, ceilings, eligible software, approved-provider process) remain **unverified**. The programme may have been replaced by the 2025 decree; this could not be confirmed.
- No evidence on whether recurring SaaS subscriptions (opex) qualify under decree 2.25.342, which reads as investment- or capex-oriented.
- The DigiTPME network's partner model (whether software vendors can join as solution providers) was not found.

## 3. Do banks or telecom operators bundle SME software or partner with SaaS startups in Morocco?

### Takeaway
**Banks are actively entering SME software themselves rather than reselling startups.** Bank of Africa launched **Hsabati Pro** (May 2026): invoicing, cash, CRM, stock and HR, **free for the first year**, positioned under the Charte TPE. Attijariwafa runs Attijari Payment (acquiring since May 2025), partners with *vertical leaders* (Sobrus) and a bill-payment fintech (MadfoatCom), and reportedly launched a neobank ("Simple.") and smartphone-as-POS ("Sahl Pay") in 2026. CIH and BCP run fintech challenges but have no SME software bundle that I could find. **Telecoms do not bundle SME software.** inwi, Orange and Maroc Telecom act as connectivity and cloud providers, startup-programme sponsors, chamber-of-commerce digitalisation partners and investors.

### Cited Findings
**Bank of Africa**
- **HSABATI PRO**, launched **May 2026**: a web platform covering invoicing, cash management, sales tracking/CRM, stock and some HR, with real-time KPIs. Targets TPE, entrepreneurs, professionals and liberal professions. **First year fully free, then a preferential rate.** Tied to BOA's **Charte TPE** commitments. — [Finances News](https://www.fnh.ma/article/actualites-marocaines/bank-of-africa-lance-hsabati-pro-plateforme-digitale-gestion-entrepreneurs-maroc); [Le Matin](https://lematin.ma/economie/bank-of-africa-lance-hsabati-pro-pour-les-entrepreneurs/344459); [Consonews](https://consonews.ma/lancements/hsabati-pro-bank-of-africa-lance-une-plateforme-digitale-pour-simplifier-la-gestion-des-entrepreneurs)
- No adoption figures were published. Whether Hsabati Pro is built in-house or white-labelled from a startup was **not disclosed** in the results. (Note: `digital_economy_and_ecosystem.md` lists a separate startup called "Hsabati", SMB management plus working capital. Whether the two are related is unconfirmed.)

- **Orange:** telecom partner to the **Club des Dirigeants** for 2025 (sponsored content); its B2B marketing pitches a sovereign cloud. Orange co-launched **DabaDoc Consult** (video consultation) and owns DabaDoc with AXA. — [Médias24 (native ad)](https://medias24.com/nativeads/4e-edition-du-digital-now-orange-conclut-une-convention-avec-le-club-des-dirigeants/); [We Are Tech](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/dabadoc-et-orange-lancent-dabadoc-consult-un-service-de-video-consultation-sur-l-afrique)
- Mobile money: Orange Money Maroc; inwi's Wana Money (2017). — [Wikipedia: Orange Morocco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_Morocco); [Hespress FR](https://fr.hespress.com/65268-65268.html)

### Inferences
- **Hsabati Pro is the single most important competitive finding.** A top-3 bank now gives TPE invoicing, cash, CRM and stock **free for a year** and can push it through its branch network and TPE account openings. A standalone SaaS selling generic "gestion commerciale" to TPE now competes against free plus the bank's distribution.
- Attijari's pattern (partner with the *vertical leader* such as Sobrus; build its own acceptance and neobank rails) suggests banks partner with startups that **already dominate a vertical**, not with early-stage generalists. Startups get bank distribution only after they win a niche.
- Telecom "partnerships" in Morocco are awareness programmes (inwi–CCIS, inwi–APEBI) and investments. They could get a vendor onto a chamber-of-commerce roadshow, but no evidence shows telcos billing or reselling third-party SaaS.

### Gaps
- Whether Hsabati Pro is white-labelled from a startup, its post-year-one price, and its uptake.
- Details of Attijari's "Simple." and "Sahl Pay" (whether they include invoicing or stock tools).
- CIH, BCP and Crédit Agricole TPE packs with management tools in 2025–26: not found.
- An FNH item on TPE and Bank of Africa with Mastercard ([fnh.ma](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-financiere-maroc/tpe-bank-africa-mastercard)) appeared in results, but its content was not retrieved.

## 4. What role do business and e-commerce influencers, trainers ("formateurs") and Facebook/TikTok groups play in software adoption?

### Takeaway
Evidence beyond YouCan's own affiliate programme (covered in the earlier notes) is **thin**. Web search does not index Facebook groups, TikTok or YouTube Darija content. What I did find: YouCan's co-founder is himself ranked as a top Moroccan business influencer, and sector associations such as FNEM package e-commerce offers. No Moroccan affiliate deal between a B2B SaaS and a formateur, other than YouCan's, was found.

### Cited Findings
- Favikon's ranking of top business influencers in Morocco includes **Mohamed El Ghaissani (YouCan co-founder)**, described as a serial entrepreneur behind a leading MEA e-commerce SaaS. — [Favikon](https://www.favikon.com/blog/top-business-influencers-morocco)
- The **Fédération nationale du e-commerce au Maroc (FNEM)**, led by El Amine Serhani Al Idrissi, offers a "pack e-commerce" and a dispute-resolution platform, and has publicly clashed with Maroc Télécommerce (date unclear, likely older). — [Finances News](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/e-commerce-ca-chauffe-entre-maroc-telecommerce-et-la-fnem)
- Searches for Moroccan formateurs and TikTok affiliation returned only French TikTok Shop content. No source confirms TikTok Shop availability in Morocco. — [JDN (France)](https://www.journaldunet.com/retail/1537961-tout-ce-qu-il-faut-savoir-sur-tiktok-shop/)
- Carried forward (earlier notes): YouCan's partner page advertises "Earn 50%", while a blog reports 20% lifetime revenue share; its events draw 4,500+ people. — [YouCan Partner Program](https://youcan.shop/en/affiliate); [ribhonline](https://ribhonline.com/blog/youcan-affiliate-maroc)

### Inferences
- In Morocco the influencer channel for software appears **founder-led and platform-owned**: YouCan's founder is the influencer and YouCan owns the affiliate network. A newcomer would be renting attention inside an ecosystem YouCan already controls. That makes it a channel for *complements* to YouCan (apps, integrations), not substitutes.
- Sector federations (FNEM; CCIS chambers via inwi and Attijari) are a more searchable, institutional version of the "community" channel.

### Gaps
- No data on Moroccan Facebook or WhatsApp group sizes, TikTok/YouTube business-trainer audiences, or affiliate commission levels outside YouCan. This needs manual social-media research, which web search cannot do.
- No example found of an accounting, POS or invoicing SaaS in Morocco paying influencers or trainers.

## 5. Do Moroccan SMEs prefer Darija/Arabic interfaces and WhatsApp support, and how do they pay for software subscriptions?

### Takeaway
The only Darija evidence remains vendor marketing plus the Sunergia language survey (earlier notes: Darija is used in 69% of professional speech but only 39% of professional writing, where French leads). Several Moroccan products already sell **WhatsApp-native, Darija-capable** sales, support and payment tools (Babliy, Dashy, ChatDaddy, eGrow). No survey covers how SMEs pay for software. The observable patterns are **annual-prepay discounts** (DabaDoc 2015), **free-first-year** offers (Hsabati Pro), and FX card limits that complicate paying foreign SaaS.

### Cited Findings
- **Babliy**: an AI sales agent on a shop's WhatsApp number, answering in **Darija, French and Arabic**, recommending from the catalog, confirming orders, with a shared inbox and human takeover; free setup in under 5 minutes (vendor). — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/p/babliy)
- **Dashy** (Moroccan, founded 2024) turns WhatsApp into a sales, support and **payment-collection** channel with secure payment links inside the chat; founder Imad Moujahid. Darija support is not stated. — [We Are Tech](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/tech-stars/maroc-imad-moujahid-fait-de-whatsapp-un-canal-de-vente-et-de-paiement)
- ChatDaddy (WhatsApp API vendor) advises **French for professional messages and Darija for casual/promotional ones**, often mixed (vendor). — [ChatDaddy](https://chatdaddy.tech/blog/whatsapp-marketing-morocco)
- Carried forward: Sunergia 2024, Darija is used in 69% of professional speech and 39% of professional writing. — [Yabiladi](https://en.yabiladi.com/articles/details/164733/darija-dominates-french-leads-professionally.html) *(carried forward from `digital_economy_and_ecosystem.md`; not re-retrieved here)*
- **Payment patterns observed:** DabaDoc's 2015 tiers of 450 / 300 / 225 MAD per month (monthly / quarterly / annual). — [TelQuel 2015](https://telquel.ma/2015/05/25/dabadoc-service-marocain-sante-en-ligne-sexporte-en-algerie-en-tunisie_1448476). Hsabati Pro is free for year one, then a preferential rate. — [Finances News](https://www.fnh.ma/article/actualites-marocaines/bank-of-africa-lance-hsabati-pro-plateforme-digitale-gestion-entrepreneurs-maroc). The FX card limit for imported services is 200k MAD/year per beneficiary for ordinary firms. — [Office des Changes](https://www.oc.gov.ma/fr/faq/les-entreprises-marocaines-peuvent-elles-regler-par-carte-de-paiement-internationale-leurs)

### Inferences
- Darija/WhatsApp-native UX is **no longer a differentiator on its own** in e-commerce and sales tooling, where at least four vendors claim it (see also `gap_ecommerce_tools.md`). It may still differentiate in back-office tools (accounting, stock, payroll), where competitors are French-UI desktop or web tools. That is inferred from competitor descriptions, not demand data.
- Pricing should offer **annual prepay with a large discount** and accept bank transfer. A bank-embedded rival is free for a year.

### Gaps
- No survey on SME interface-language preference or on payment method for software (card vs transfer vs cash vs mobile wallet).
- No data on MT Cash, Orange Money or inwi money use for B2B subscriptions.

## 6. Adversarial test: is the opportunity really underserved?

### Takeaway
**Partly served, and better served than the earlier notes implied.** The proven channels (field sales into hanouts, government shop-digitisation deals, bank distribution, the e-commerce trainer and affiliate network) are **already held by funded incumbents**. The assumed cheap channels (80–90% Mowakaba subsidy, telecom bundles) **could not be verified or do not exist**. Generic TPE invoicing and management now faces a **free-for-a-year bank product (BOA Hsabati Pro)** on top of a crowded field of local tools at 60–500 MAD/month. Real openings remain in verticals without a dominant player and in micro-firm e-invoicing compliance (if the decree lands). A newcomer should not count on any distribution shortcut.

### Inferences
- **Well served:** hanout retail tech, pharmacies, doctor booking, COD e-commerce platforms, and WhatsApp sales bots.
- **Crowded and now bank-contested:** generic TPE invoicing and commercial management.
- **Plausibly still underserved (not disproven here):** verticals with no named leader in these results (see `vertical_niches.md`: syndics, car rental, beauty salons, schools); fiduciaire-mediated e-invoicing for micro-firms and auto-entrepreneurs in 2027+; and back-office tools in Darija/Arabic. All of these depend on demand evidence beyond this file's scope.
- **Distribution implications:** expect founder-led field or inside sales and 2–5 years to reach 1,000 paying customers (DabaDoc, LaCaisse precedent). The most realistic leverage points are (a) integrations with platforms that own the flow (Glovo→LaCaisse, Shopify/YouCan→Sendit, bank PSPs→Sobrus); (b) chambers of commerce and ADD DigiTPME events; (c) fiduciaires. Subsidy and telecom shortcuts should be removed from the plan.

### Gaps
- No evidence on Hsabati Pro's actual uptake or quality. If it is weak, the bank threat is overstated.
- A Moroccan Odoo/Zoho/Sage partner-channel census was not done (search budget).
- Absence of evidence on Facebook/TikTok group channels reflects the search tool's limits, not proof that the channel is absent.

## 1. Size and structure (TPE/PME/GE, auto-entrepreneurs, informal sector, sectors, geography, creations and failures)

### Takeaway
Morocco has about 380k formal companies (legal persons) and about 463k registered auto-entrepreneurs. The informal sector is far larger, with about 2.03M informal production units. The formal base is overwhelmingly micro-sized: about 87% have under MAD 3M turnover and about 94% under MAD 10M. It is concentrated in Casablanca-Settat (about 38% of companies, about 60% of turnover) and dominated by commerce and construction. About 110k companies are created each year, but failures (about 15k a year) and dissolutions are high, and an estimated 300k "zombie" companies carry the risk.

### Cited Findings
**Formal companies (legal persons): stock and size**
- In 2024 Morocco had **380,230 companies that are legal persons (sociétés)**, up 1.3% year on year. Source: the 6th annual OMTPME report (data to end-2024), presented in Casablanca on 7 Jan 2026. — [Hespress](https://fr.hespress.com/457430-omtpme-tissu-entrepreneurial-en-croissance-en-2024-mais-sous-forte-pression.html); [TelQuel](https://telquel.ma/instant-t/2026/01/07/tpme-un-tissu-productif-resilient-malgre-certaines-disparites_1969107/); [Finances News](https://www.fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/tpme-dynamique-fragilite-persistante); [OMTPME Rapport annuel Édition 2025 (PDF)](https://omtpme.ma/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/OMTPME_RA-Edition-2025.pdf)
- About **94% of these companies have turnover below MAD 10M**. "PME in the strict sense" are only **5.5%** of the total. — [Hespress](https://fr.hespress.com/457430-omtpme-tissu-entrepreneurial-en-croissance-en-2024-mais-sous-forte-pression.html)
- OMTPME size definitions by annual turnover: **Micro ≤ MAD 3M** and **TPE (VSE) MAD 3M to under 10M**. PME and GE sit above these thresholds. — [OMTPME Annual Report 2024 (EN, PDF)](https://omtpme.ma/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Annual-Report-2024_-OMTPME.pdf)

**Informal sector (HCP ENSI 2023/2024, published May 2025)**
- About **2.03 million informal production units (UPI) in 2023**, about 353,000 more than in 2014. — [HCP synthesis (PDF)](https://m.hcp.ma/file/244334); [TelQuel](https://telquel.ma/instant-t/2025/05/28/au-maroc-le-nombre-dentreprises-informelles-a-augmente-en-dix-ans_1935062/)

### Inferences
- The addressable formal population is roughly 380k companies plus 380–463k AEs plus formally registered sole traders (natural persons; no stock figure found), so about 0.8–1M formal units. The 2.03M informal units are mostly one-person commerce and services and are unlikely to pay for SaaS. They may still be reachable through free or payment-led tools such as a digital karne or a POS.
- About 87% of companies earn under MAD 3M, so most targets are micro companies with small budgets. The PME core that could pay for mid-market SaaS is small: 5.5% of 380k is about 21k companies.
- Casablanca prefecture alone holds more than 86% of the region's companies and the region is 37.8% of the national total. That implies roughly a third of Morocco's formal companies sit in one city. A Casablanca-first go-to-market covers a large share of the market.
- High churn (about 110k creations a year against about 15k failures, rising dissolutions and about 300k zombies) means a large flow of new companies each year. That flow is an acquisition channel: register-and-equip at creation, through OMPIC/CRI, banks and fiduciaires. It also means a high natural customer-churn floor for SaaS.

### Gaps
- No official 2024–2026 count of registered sole traders (natural persons) as a stock, only flows.
- No national breakdown of the stock of companies that are legal persons by sector or region for Rabat-Salé-Kénitra, Tanger-Tétouan-Al Hoceima, Marrakech-Safi, Souss-Massa (Agadir) or Fès-Meknès. Regional OMTPME PDFs exist ([Marrakech-Safi](https://omtpme.ma/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Rapport-regional-Marrakech-Safi_edition-2024.pdf), [RSK](https://omtpme.ma/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Rapport-regional-RSK_edition-2024.pdf)), but their contents could not be read.
- No primary Inforisk release for 2025 was read; the figures come via Finances News.

## 2. Digital maturity (computers, accounting software, ERP, CRM, e-invoicing, websites, social media, online payments)

### Takeaway
Hard, recent, official data on SME software adoption does not exist publicly. The last HCP measurement is from 2019/2020: 81% of TPE online, 59% of companies using specialised business software, 31% with a website. Vendor-sourced estimates, which are unverified, suggest most SMEs still run on Excel and only about 10% use an ERP. Card acceptance among merchants is very low (about 4–5% of addressable merchants) and cash dominates. The biggest near-term forcing function is DGI B2B e-invoicing (clearance model), which reaches large companies in 2026 and SMEs, TPE and AEs from 1 Jan 2027. Its decree and thresholds were not final as of mid-2026.

### Cited Findings
**Computer, internet and software use (HCP; data 2019/2020, published Feb 2022)**
- Internet access in 2019: **81% of TPE, 97.9% of PME and 99.5% of large companies**. Email is the main use (92%). **Only 59% of companies use specialised professional software**, and 38% do not use online document access or editing. — [HCP, Intégration des TIC au niveau entreprises](https://www.hcp.ma/Integration-des-TIC-au-niveau-entreprises-au-Maroc_a3370.html); [HCP PDF](https://hcp.ma/file/233201)
- **Only 31% of companies have a website** (HCP, 2019 data). — [Le Matin 2022](https://lematin.ma/express/2022/integration-tic-seules-31-entreprises-maroc-disposent-d-site-web/371399.html)
- Survey base: 2,101 companies in industry, construction, commerce and non-financial services (2019), plus 3,600 companies in Dec 2020. — [HCP](https://www.hcp.ma/Integration-des-TIC-au-niveau-entreprises-au-Maroc_a3370.html)
- Older data (Jan 2019, Officium/ONPM): **30% of SMEs were active in digital projects and 30% did nothing digital**. — [Finances News](https://www.fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/l-apebi-lance-une-consultation-publique-sur-la-transition-digitale-du-maroc)

**Management-software penetration (vendor and commercial sources, low reliability)**
- Vendor guides (2026) claim that **about 70% of Moroccan SMEs "manage everything in Excel"**, that **only 30–35% use a digital management tool beyond spreadsheets**, that **fewer than 20% have a professional website**, and that **about 10% use an ERP**. None of these figures has a traceable primary source. — [Oasis Technocloud](https://oasistechnocloud.com/blog/digitalisation-pme-maroc/); [Tourak Digital](https://tourakdigital.ma/blog/transformation-digitale-pme-maroc)
- Sage Maroc / Immersion study (published Jan 2024): in-person interviews, including with **107 experts-comptables**. It found that **company size is the only factor that effectively determines whether a company is equipped with management software**, and that **two out of three companies with a management solution use Sage**. — [TelQuel, 10 Jan 2024](https://telquel.ma/2024/01/10/sage-publie-sa-radioscopie-des-solutions-de-gestion-au-maroc_1850334)

**World Bank Enterprise Survey 2023 (raw, unweighted counts)**
- Of 598 respondents on access to finance, about 27% rated it a major or very severe obstacle. As the *single biggest obstacle*, corruption was named 108 times and informal-sector competitors 97 times, against only 17 for finance. The catalogue warns that these are unweighted counts. — [World Bank Microdata, Morocco ES 2023](https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/6455/variable/F1/V225?name=k30)
- A Bank Al-Maghrib working paper (Apr 2026) pooled Enterprise Survey data from 11 MENA countries (Morocco: 1,860 observations, 2013/2019/2023). It identifies political instability, access to finance and informal competition as the binding constraints. — [Morocco World News](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2026/04/289021/what-blocks-mena-firms-from-growing-a-bank-al-maghrib-study-responds/)

**Payments acceptance (merchants)**
- More than 21M bank cards in circulation at end-2025, and card-terminal (TPE) payments are growing. — [Finances News](https://fnh.ma/articles/paiement-electronique-maroc-sortie-tout-cash)
- **Only about 4–5% of the about 1.2M "addressable" merchants have a card terminal** (Visa / Switch Al-Maghrib analysis; sponsored content). — [Médias24 (native ad)](https://medias24.com/nativeads/debat-la-can-2025-catalyseur-de-la-digitalisation-des-paiements-au-maroc/)
- 13.7M active e-wallets, but commercial (merchant) use is only about 6% of wallet transactions (Dec 2025). — [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/le-paiement-mobile-explose-au-maroc-et-devient-un-levier-dinclusion-financiere_4FGXD5CZNZHHRL6O323P2A4P6E/)
- About **89% of hanout transactions are still in cash**. — [LaunchBase Africa](https://launchbaseafrica.com/?p=11208)
- Bank Al-Maghrib is cutting the interchange cap from 0.65% to **0.50%, effective 1 Oct 2026**. — [Bladi.net](https://www.bladi.net/payer-carte-coutera-cher-maroc,122427.html)
- Attijari Payment was licensed as a payment institution in Mar 2025 and started merchant acquiring on 1 May 2025. — [Attijari Payment press release (PDF)](https://www.attijariwafabank.com/sites/default/files/publication/2025-09/CP%20VF%20-%20AP%20acquisition%20commer%C3%A7ants.pdf)
- CMI's **Fatourati** bill-payment platform processed **more than 218M multichannel bill/tax/fee payment operations in 2024**. — [Finances News](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/fatourati-plateforme-maroc-factures)

**E-invoicing (DGI)**
- Morocco chose a **"clearance" model**: each invoice must be validated by the DGI platform before it is legally issued. The scope is **B2B first, with no B2C** at this stage. — [Sage Maroc blog](https://www.sage.com/fr-ma/blog/facturation-electronique-maroc-2026/); [Lembra Conseil](https://lembra-conseil.com/facturation-electronique-au-maroc-guide-complet-2026/)
- Announced phasing (secondary sources, thresholds differ): **large companies from 1 Jan 2026** (more than MAD 200M per Experio); **mid-size from 1 Jul 2026** (MAD 10–200M); **PME, TPE and AEs from 1 Jan 2027**. Experio puts the AE/small threshold at more than MAD 500k, while Lembra/Inyad cite TPE under MAD 10M and AEs above MAD 500k. — [Experio](https://experio.ma/facturation-electronique-maroc-2026-guide-conformite/); [Lembra Conseil](https://lembra-conseil.com/facturation-electronique-au-maroc-guide-complet-2026/); [Inyad](https://inyad.com/blog/calendrier-obligation-facturation-electronique-maroc)
- **The implementing decree was still pending**: a draft was transmitted in Apr 2026 per Experio, and Hisab/Mohammed Teto says it was at the SGG. Formats are structured UBL 2.1 or CII XML, and plain PDF is excluded. The sanction cited by Experio is **MAD 500 per non-compliant invoice**, not verified in an official text. The legal basis is CGI Art. 145-IX, as accelerated by the 2024 Finance Law. — [Experio](https://experio.ma/facturation-electronique-maroc-2026-guide-conformite/); [Mohammed Teto](https://mohammedteto.com/blog/facturation-electronique-maroc/); [Inyad](https://inyad.com/blog/facture-electronique-maroc-2026)

**Social media**
- No study measures SME or merchant use of WhatsApp Business or Instagram. Consumer use in 2025 (Sunergia barometer, 1,009 people, Casablanca): **WhatsApp 75%, Facebook 62%, Instagram 42%**. In 2026, 86% use at least one social network, up from 81% in 2025. — [TelQuel](https://telquel.ma/instant-t/2025/05/30/reseaux-sociaux-au-maroc-whatsapp-facebook-et-instagram-dominent-19-des-marocains-toujours-hors-ligne_1935598/); [Hespress](https://fr.hespress.com/488049-au-maroc-whatsapp-domine-toujours-les-reseaux-sociaux-tandis-que-tiktok-accelere-sa-progression.html)

### Inferences
- E-invoicing in 2027 is the strongest software-adoption trigger the Moroccan SME base has ever faced. Several hundred thousand companies, plus AEs above the threshold, will need a DGI-connected invoicing tool. Almost every local vendor already markets "DGI 2026 compliant", so compliance alone will not differentiate a product, and execution and price will.
- With merchant card acceptance at about 4–5% and a hanout economy that is about 89% cash, tools that assume online payment rails (Stripe-style billing, payment links) face a small current base. The base is growing quickly because of new payment institutions (Attijari Payment, Chari) and lower interchange.
- WhatsApp is the dominant channel (75% of consumers). Any SME tool (invoicing, reminders, catalogue, customer support) that works through WhatsApp aligns with existing behaviour. This is an inference; no SME-specific data was found.

### Gaps
- No 2023–2026 official survey (HCP, APEBI, CGEM, Maroc PME, ADD) gives the share of SMEs using accounting software, ERP, CRM or online payments. APEBI does not appear to publish a recurring digital barometer.
- No weighted World Bank ES 2023 indicators for website, email or online-sales use could be retrieved.
- No McKinsey, BCG or Deloitte Morocco-specific SME digitalisation study was found within the search budget.

## 3. Top pain points of Moroccan small-business owners

### Takeaway
The best-evidenced pain is **cash flow from late payment**: TPE wait about 100 days on average to be paid, the longest of any size class. Next come **lack of access to bank credit** (TPE get about 20% of bank credit outstanding) and a **tax, CNSS and administrative burden** that small-business federations call ill-adapted to small structures. Informal competition is named more often than finance as the single biggest obstacle in the 2023 World Bank survey. Customer credit ("karne") is endemic in proximity retail.

### Cited Findings
**Late payments and cash flow**
- The late-payment observatory, using 2024 data, found that **TPE cut their customer payment delays by about 30 days but still have the longest average, about 100 days of revenue**. TPE also cut their supplier delays by 15 days, against 6 days for PME. — [EcoActu](https://ecoactu.ma/delais-de-paiement-le-maroc-reduit-lecart-avec-la-loi/); [LesEco](https://leseco.ma/business/delais-de-paiement-les-grandes-structures-concentrent-encore-lessentiel-des-retards.html)
- Another article says TPE **collect in 130 days on average and pay in 83 days**. The reference period is unspecified, so this conflicts with the 2024 figure. — [LesEco](https://leseco.ma/maroc/delais-de-paiement-les-tpe-ces-grandes-oubliees.html)
- Public establishments (EEP): average payment time **32.5 days at end-2025**, against 55.9 days in Dec 2018, with the curve flat since 2023. **EEPs paid MAD 423.2M in late-payment fines in 2025**, down 41.2% from MAD 720M in 2024, and 65 entities were sanctioned. **Public plus private fines totalled MAD 1.331bn.** — [Le Matin](https://lematin.ma/economie/delais-de-paiement-423-mdh-damendes-payes-par-les-eep-en-2025/367839); [Libération](https://libe.ma/delais-de-paiement-le-secteur-public-premier-contrevenant-de-ses-propres-regles/)
- Press commentary says **more than half of TPE suffer delays beyond 90 days**. — [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/tpe-une-crise-systemique-qui-menace-leconomie_ZPFXJJOC2VAFFG43AFKYLLA25Q/)
- Allianz Trade: **DSO of 90–120 days on average**, with domestic payment behaviour "worsening" (page undated). An older edition said 120–150 days. Morocco scored **56/100 ("very high" collection complexity)** in the 4th Collection Complexity Index (Jan 2026). Legal action is "unreasonable in most cases" and insolvency proceedings are slow and inefficient. — [Allianz Trade Morocco](https://www.allianz-trade.com/en_global/economic-research/collection-complexity/morocco.html); [Le Matin](https://lematin.ma/economie/pourquoi-le-maroc-reste-un-pays-complexe-pour-le-recouvrement-des-creances/327070)
- Prof. Mohamed Amrani credited the late-payment law (Law 69-21) with easing company cash flow in 2025. — [Finances News](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/defaillances-entreprises-baisse-faillites)
- A Bank Al-Maghrib analysis of microenterprises and TPE says **60% of companies face cash-flow problems** (survey date not visible). — [Finances News](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-financiere-maroc/microentreprises-et-tpe-les-freins-a-l-inclusion-financiere)

**Access to credit**
- Bank credit outstanding: **TPE 20%, PME 21%, large companies 60%** (OMTPME, 2024 data). Total outstanding is about MAD 630.4bn. — [Hespress](https://fr.hespress.com/457430-omtpme-tissu-entrepreneurial-en-croissance-en-2024-mais-sous-forte-pression.html); [Le Matin](https://lematin.ma/economie/ce-que-revele-le-rapport-2025-de-lobservatoire-marocain-de-la-tpme/323229)
- CM-TPME and the press claim that **fewer than 5% of TPE obtain classic bank credit** for lack of collateral or structured accounts. The primary source is not verified. — [LeBrief](https://www.lebrief.ma/tpe-marocaines-la-cm-tpme-alerte-sur-une-crise-de-survie-100157436/)
- EIB/EU survey (Potloc, May 2025, 150 SME owners): **49% had received a formal loan**, and **27% cited a lack of support with administrative procedures**. Headline: "half of Moroccan SMEs remain outside the banking system". — [Finances News](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-financiere-maroc/pme-financement-pme-marocaines)
- A **TPE financing charter was signed on 4 Dec 2025**, and banks committed **about MAD 18bn to TPE in 2026**. Tamwilcom refinancing runs at 25 bp below the policy rate. Cash-flow facilities to TPME were still down 4.3%. — [BAM Council press release Dec 2025 (PDF)](https://www.bkam.ma/content/download/836202/9086535/Communiqu%C3%A9_ConseilBAM_d%C3%A9cembre%202025_Fr.pdf); [LesEco](https://leseco.ma/business/financement-de-la-tpe-bank-al-maghrib-active-le-levier-de-la-garantie.html)
- Bank Al-Maghrib notes that **new companies struggle to finance their start-up phase because they are hard to score**. — [Finances News](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-financiere-maroc/microentreprises-et-tpe-les-freins-a-l-inclusion-financiere)

**Tax, CNSS and administrative burden**
- The CM-TPME study "Le moteur enrayé" (Mar 2026) finds **taxation poorly adapted to small structures** and **administrative procedures too heavy and costly**. It flags a possible rise in **TPE corporate tax from 10% to 20% in 2026** and says CNSS and tax decisions are taken without TPE representation. It makes 25 proposals, including a simplified tax regime. — [LeBrief](https://www.lebrief.ma/tpe-marocaines-la-cm-tpme-alerte-sur-une-crise-de-survie-100157436/)
- Payroll compliance load in 2026: **CNSS/AMO total 21.09% for the employer and 6.74% for the employee**, and **IR is progressive from 0% to 37%**. The monthly **Damancom** CNSS declaration is due before the 10th, and **SIMPL-IR** by the end of the following month. Required software features: Damancom compatibility, DNS file generation and SIMPL integration. — [Upsilon Consulting](https://upsilon-consulting.com/comparatif-logiciels-paie-maroc/); [Mercans](https://mercans.com/global-payroll/morocco/)
- CESE report (Nov 2025) on the challenges facing micro, very small and small companies. Contents were not readable in this session. — [CESE (PDF)](https://www.cese.ma/media/2025/11/Les-defis-des-micro-tres-petites-et-petites-entreprises-au-Maroc-.pdf)

**Informal competition**
- In the World Bank ES 2023, "practices of competitors in the informal sector" is the **second most-cited single biggest obstacle** (97 of 598 raw responses, behind corruption at 108). — [World Bank Microdata](https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/6455/variable/F1/V225?name=k30)

**Customer credit at hanouts ("karne")**
- Chari acquired **Karny.ma**, a virtual credit notebook said to be **used by more than 50,000 points of sale**. — [Finances News](https://fnh.ma/article/actualites-marocaines/apres-sa-levee-de-fonds-chari-ma-annonce-l-acquisition-de-l-application-de-carnet-de-credit-karny-ma)
- Hanouty launched a deferred-payment card to replace the paper credit notebook (around 2008–09, older data). — [Bladi.net](https://www.bladi.net/hanouty-superettes.html)
- Hanout counts: about **126,000 hanouts nationwide**, accounting for more than 85% of food spending (LaunchBase Africa). An older estimate was about 90,000. Proximity commerce employs about **1.3M merchants** (Finances News). — [LaunchBase Africa](https://launchbaseafrica.com/?p=11208); [Yabiladi (older)](https://www.yabiladi.com/article-economie-1059.html); [Finances News](https://www.fnh.ma/articles/commerce-proximite-digitalisation)

### Inferences
- The pains map to software in this order of evidence strength: (1) receivables, collections and dunning, plus cash-flow forecasting; (2) "bankable" bookkeeping that produces credit-ready statements, given that BAM explicitly links poor scoring to the lack of structured accounts; (3) compliance automation for e-invoicing, payroll (Damancom, SIMPL) and tax; (4) digital customer credit for retail.
- The late-payment law publishes fines and statistics, which may make "late-payment tracking and interest computation" a sellable compliance and cash-recovery feature. This is an inference.

### Gaps
- No primary owner survey with ranked pain points (for example a CGEM or Maroc PME barometer for 2024–2026) was found, and no direct owner quotes were retrieved.
- Inventory management and customer-acquisition pains are not quantified in any source found.

## 4. Role of accountants (experts-comptables, comptables agréés, fiduciaires) and their potential as a SaaS channel

### Takeaway
There are only about **887 chartered experts-comptables** (2026), plus a few thousand comptables agréés and many unregulated "fiduciaires", serving about 380k companies. Small companies outsource bookkeeping almost entirely to this fragmented layer. Sage dominates the equipped segment and already runs an accountant-pricing channel. Accountants are a credible but contested distribution channel for SME SaaS.

### Cited Findings
- **887 experts-comptables registered with the Ordre at 30 Mar 2026, representing 609 legal entities** (firms). This was also the voter base for the OEC elections of 21 May 2026. — [Médias24](https://medias24.com/2026/04/23/ordre-des-experts-comptables-elections-sur-fond-de-reflexion-sur-louverture-du-capital-1665029/); [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/elections-de-lordre-des-experts-comptables-suspense-total-a-la-veille-du-scrutin-du-21-mai_GGPN73XPTBANHPM7FT66B3DMA4/)
- 867 members were cited at the Nov 2025 OEC congress. — [Le Matin](https://lematin.ma/economie/congres-oec-a-rabat-sur-lia-la-durabilite-et-les-talents/310313)
- Fayçal Mekouar (Apr 2026): **"Only 2% of companies have certified accounts."** — [Médias24](https://medias24.com/2026/04/12/faycal-mekouar-2-des-entreprises-seulement-ont-des-comptes-certifies-est-ce-normal-1658424/)
- The profession is governed by Law 15-89 (1993) and has six regional councils, heavily concentrated in Casablanca. Its limits as a system are discussed in LesEco. — [LesEco](https://leseco.ma/maroc/experts-comptables-les-limites-du-systeme-au-maroc.html)
- **Comptables agréés** (Law 127-12, amended by Law 53-19) may keep and close accounts but cannot certify them. Counts: **1,665 registered comptables agréés and about 2,400 providers without status** (date not shown). In 2019, 1,757 professionals received the title automatically and 2,139 did not meet the conditions. Another reference cites 3,934 entries on Ministry of Finance lists (scope and year unclear). — [Finances News](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/comptables-agrees-la-profession-mieux-encadree); [Médias24 2019](https://medias24.com/2019/06/27/comptables-agrees-benchaaboun-assouplit-les-conditions-dacces/); [La Vie Éco](https://www.lavieeco.com/?p=48368)
- "Fiduciaire", "cabinet comptable" and "bureau de gestion" are **not protected titles**, so unregistered operators exist. The profession reports falling revenue and competition from unauthorised actors. — [Upsilon Consulting](https://upsilon-consulting.com/choisir-expert-comptable-maroc/); [La Vie Éco](https://www.lavieeco.com/?p=110434)
- Software used by accountants: the Sage/Immersion study (Jan 2024) interviewed 107 experts-comptables and found that **2 of 3 equipped companies use Sage**. A Sage reseller advertises **"tarifs privilégiés pour experts-comptables 2025"**, which shows that Sage already runs an accountant-partner channel. — [TelQuel](https://telquel.ma/2024/01/10/sage-publie-sa-radioscopie-des-solutions-de-gestion-au-maroc_1850334); [Nodma Horizon (Sage reseller)](https://nodmahorizon.ma/nodma/sage/)
- The Moroccan comparison guides most often cite Sage, EBP, Odoo and Claris as the accounting packages. — [Claris blog](https://www.claris.ma/blog/logiciel-comptabilite-maroc-comparatif/)

### Inferences
- About 4–5k regulated accountants (887 EC plus about 1.7–3.9k comptables agréés), plus an unregulated fiduciaire tail, against about 380k companies and 460k AEs, means each accountant has a large book of micro clients. Collaboration SaaS on the "accountant hub plus client portal" model (document collection, e-invoice ingestion, Damancom/SIMPL filing) has strong leverage per sale.
- Accountants face revenue pressure and e-invoicing will automate data capture, so tools that let fiduciaires upsell advisory or handle more clients per head may be welcome. Sage's incumbency inside accounting firms is the main obstacle.

### Gaps
- No current (2025–26) official count of comptables agréés (OPCA register) or of operating fiduciaires was found.
- No survey gives the share of TPE that outsource accounting, the typical monthly fiduciaire fee in MAD, or which software fiduciaires use beyond Sage.

## 5. Current software landscape and competitors (accounting/invoicing, POS/retail, HR/payroll, CRM/ERP, bank tools)

### Takeaway
The landscape is crowded at the low end with many small local invoicing and commercial-management SaaS products, typically **MAD 60–500 a month**, all racing to claim "DGI e-invoicing compliance". Sage is the incumbent in accounting and Odoo is spreading through integrators. In retail, Chari (backed by a payment licence) and state-backed z.systems/Woliz are digitising hanouts. Banks have begun bundling free management tools: Bank of Africa's Hsabati Pro is free in year one. Payroll SaaS is mostly priced on quotation. No Moroccan SME-software user reviews were found.

## 6. Willingness and ability to pay

### Takeaway
Observed list prices put the accepted range for micro and TPE SaaS at roughly **MAD 60–300 a month** (about US$6–30), with PME ERP tools at **MAD 6.5–10k a year**. Ability to pay is constrained: about 87% of companies earn under MAD 3M, and about 73% of CNSS-declared employees earn under MAD 4,000 a month. Free tiers and bank give-aways anchor expectations low. Historical piracy was high (66% of software in 2008), but no recent figure exists. No survey measures the preference for subscription versus licence or the B2B payment methods used.

### Cited Findings
- Price points: InvoiceGenAI MAD 80; Facturis MAD 99 and 279; Facturaty MAD 99 and 249; ETManager MAD 60–320; Webnox MAD 149–495; Sogress from MAD 149 a month; Manageo MAD 6,500–10,000 a year; LINEPROD about MAD 7,500–9,900 a year; Odoo Enterprise about MAD 280 per user. — [invoicegenai.ma](https://invoicegenai.ma/tarifs); [Facturis](https://site.facturis.ma/tarifs/); [Facturaty](https://facturaty.ma/); [deadline.ma](https://deadline.ma/conseils/systemes-erp-crm/erp-marocains-editeurs-logiciels-gestion); [Sogress](https://sogress.com/); [Oasis Technocloud](https://oasistechnocloud.com/blog/logiciel-facturation-maroc/)
- Free entry points: InvoiceGenAI has a free tier of 5 invoices a month, Bank of Africa's Hsabati Pro is free for year one, and Odoo Community is free. — [invoicegenai.ma](https://invoicegenai.ma/tarifs); [Finances News](https://www.fnh.ma/article/actualites-marocaines/bank-of-africa-lance-hsabati-pro-plateforme-digitale-gestion-entrepreneurs-maroc); [Oasis Technocloud](https://oasistechnocloud.com/blog/logiciel-facturation-maroc/)
- Ability to pay: microenterprises (≤ MAD 3M turnover) are 86.7% of companies that are legal persons (2023), and about 73% of CNSS-declared employees earn under MAD 4,000 a month. — [CESE (PDF)](https://www.cese.ma/media/2025/11/Rapport-MICRO-PE-TPE-VF-25.pdf); [Aujourd'hui le Maroc](https://aujourdhui.ma/societe/rapport-annuel-de-lobservatoire-marocain-de-la-tpme-pres-de-73-des-employes-declares-a-la-cnss-ont-un-salaire-inferieur-a-4-000-dh)
- Piracy (older data only): **73% of software in Morocco was pirated** (IDC for BSA, 2003–04). Microsoft Maroc cited **66% in 2008**, against 60% in 2000. MEA was 56% and the world 36% (BSA-IDC). — [Aujourd'hui le Maroc (various BSA items)](https://aujourdhui.ma/?p=21023); [Aujourd'hui le Maroc](https://aujourdhui.ma/focus/ben-bahtane-le-piratage-est-un-frein-a-la-creation-16486)
- Sage's subscription model is tacitly renewed every 12 months (French product sheet; Moroccan terms may differ). — [Yad / Sage 100 product sheet (FR)](https://www.yad.fr/wp-content/uploads/Fiche-produit-SAGE-100-ENTREPRISE.pdf)
- Public subsidy: a vendor guide claims Maroc PME's **MOWAKABA** programme can co-finance **70–90% of SME digitalisation costs**. This is unverified with the official source. — [Oasis Technocloud](https://oasistechnocloud.com/blog/digitalisation-pme-maroc/)

### Inferences
- Pricing for micro, TPE and AE customers likely needs a free or very low entry tier (about MAD 50–100 a month) with upgrades tied to compliance (e-invoicing volume) or payments revenue. Mid-market tools can charge MAD 250–500 a month or MAD 6–10k a year.
- Bundling with something that pays for itself, such as faster collections, payment acceptance margin or credit access, will probably beat a pure subscription for TPE. Banks giving tools away reinforces this.
- Subsidies (Maroc PME programmes) may shift willingness to pay if vendors are on approved lists. This is unverified.

### Gaps
- No 2023–2026 survey on SaaS willingness to pay, subscription versus perpetual-licence preference, or the B2B payment methods used for software (card, bank transfer, cash, cheque).
- No recent (post-2018) piracy rate for Morocco.
- No data on churn or retention rates of local SME SaaS.

## 7. Gaps in the market: missing, poorly localised or over-priced tools

### Takeaway
The evidence points to under-served needs around **receivables and late-payment management**, **e-invoicing compliance for the long tail** of TPE and AEs from 2027, **payroll and CNSS compliance with transparent self-serve pricing**, **digital customer-credit (karne) and cash-first POS for proximity retail**, and **accountant–client collaboration**. Almost no direct evidence covers Darija or Arabic localisation demand; this remains an inference.

### Cited Findings
- **E-invoicing for small taxpayers**: PME, TPE and AEs fall under the DGI clearance regime from 1 Jan 2027 (thresholds unsettled), with structured XML (UBL/CII) required and PDF excluded. — [Experio](https://experio.ma/facturation-electronique-maroc-2026-guide-conformite/); [Lembra Conseil](https://lembra-conseil.com/facturation-electronique-au-maroc-guide-complet-2026/)
- **Receivables**: TPE wait about 100 days to be paid (2024), and Allianz Trade rates Morocco's collection complexity as "very high" (56/100), with courts rarely worth using. — [EcoActu](https://ecoactu.ma/delais-de-paiement-le-maroc-reduit-lecart-avec-la-loi/); [Allianz Trade](https://www.allianz-trade.com/en_global/economic-research/collection-complexity/morocco.html)
- **Payroll SaaS** pricing is opaque ("sur demande"), and compliance requires Damancom, SIMPL-IR and DNS file generation. — [Humantal](https://humantal.ma/tarifs); [Upsilon](https://upsilon-consulting.com/comparatif-logiciels-paie-maroc/)
- **Proximity retail**: about 126k hanouts that are about 89% cash; Karny's more than 50k points of sale show demand for a digital karne; state targets are 50k retailers digitised by 2030 (z.systems) and 90k merchants on Woliz Pro. — [LaunchBase Africa](https://launchbaseafrica.com/?p=11208); [Finances News](https://fnh.ma/article/actualites-marocaines/apres-sa-levee-de-fonds-chari-ma-annonce-l-acquisition-de-l-application-de-carnet-de-credit-karny-ma); [Le Desk](https://ledesk.ma/encontinu/commerce-de-proximite-la-tutelle-sappuie-sur-z-systems-et-woliz-solution-pour-accelerer-la-digitalisation/)
- **Bankability**: BAM says young companies are hard to score, and CM-TPME says TPE lack structured accounts to access credit. — [Finances News](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-financiere-maroc/microentreprises-et-tpe-les-freins-a-l-inclusion-financiere); [LeBrief](https://www.lebrief.ma/tpe-marocaines-la-cm-tpme-alerte-sur-une-crise-de-survie-100157436/)
- **Localisation**: only one local vendor (Inyad) was seen explicitly marketing a French/Arabic interface. ### Inferences
- **Most promising horizontal gaps**, ranked on the evidence:
  1. A receivables, collections and late-payment toolkit (reminders over WhatsApp and SMS, late-interest calculation under Law 69-21, payment links). It addresses the best-documented pain and few dedicated local tools were found.
  2. A low-cost e-invoicing and bookkeeping hub for TPE and AEs that plugs into the fiduciaire's workflow, distributed through accountants and banks rather than direct.
  3. Self-serve payroll and CNSS SaaS with public pricing for companies with 1–20 employees.
- Pure invoicing or POS for hanouts is already contested by well-funded or state-backed players (Chari, z.systems, Woliz, Inyad, banks). A new entrant would probably need to partner rather than compete.
- Darija/Arabic interfaces and WhatsApp-native workflows are plausible differentiators for TPE and AE owners, given WhatsApp's 75% consumer usage and the low English and moderate French literacy of many small traders. However, no Morocco-specific evidence on language preference for business software was found.

### Gaps
- No SME survey or user reviews show which existing tools disappoint, or why (price, language, tax-rule errors, support).
- No data on CRM adoption or CRM vendors among Moroccan SMEs.
- No evidence on demand for Arabic or Darija business software interfaces.
